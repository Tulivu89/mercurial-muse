# Feature sweep: brief

Nov 18, 2026 · @AmbiProp

What do the other roleplay apps let a player do that Inkwyrm doesn't, and which of those are worth having? This is the last of the planned reviews (`docs/reviews/README.md`). The owner's answer on value (`docs/value.md`) sets the test: no single feature is the draw, but the combination is. A feature earns its place if it makes a part good, or joins the parts up.

## What Inkwyrm has now (Nov 18)

**Chat:**
- **Replies:**
  - step between alternative replies, regenerate, continue, edit, delete, copy;
  - carrying on at the limit automatically;
  - a line under a reply that says why it stopped;
  - notes in brackets that set the scene without asking for a reply.
- **Memory:** journal, diary, feelings, plans, moods; chapters for what leaves the window, folded into Earlier; memory brought between chats.
- **Time:** the clock and story time, schedules with a week view, outfits and rooms that follow the schedule.
- **Personas,** and a Collection with shelves, favourites and archive.
- **Home:**
  - households with rooms and needs;
  - actions and a bond;
  - posts with comments, and the line a photo would be made from.
- **Pictures and looks:** portraits with an emotion each, moving portraits, and looks (world skins).
- **Files:**
  - card import (V1 to V3, PNG, CHARX, CharacterRoom) and export (a card picture);
  - lorebooks in and out;
  - SillyTavern chats in and out;
  - test packs and the chat lab.
- **Prompting:** the Prompt settings; models by family, with prompt formats; Gemini direct; thinking levels; the 18+ question.

**Studio:**
- worlds of records with reaches, keys, conditions and sets;
- rules on every record, threads with milestones, maps with travel, and Checks;
- the helper: Errata, drafts and Write;
- In context now;
- world files, cards and lorebooks.

**Adventure:**
- a rules engine with the four approaches, rolls and stakes;
- clock, maps and travel, threads, journal, saves;
- prompt formats per family.

**Planned, not built:**
- group chat and Stage mode (`docs/model-jobs.md`, G1 onward);
- a model per job: Voice, Side, Build;
- images through the player's own provider (roadmap step 7);
- accounts and sync (step 8), partner models (step 9);
- the Discord bot carrying characters.

## Read for this review

SillyTavern 1.19.0 (extensions, group chat, slash commands), Agnai, RisuAI, Marinara Engine, Talemate. Ideas only, never code.
