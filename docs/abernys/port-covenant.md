## The Wailing and the arrival

### The Wailing

A plague swept Abernys and struck every species. It came fast and hard with deadly fevers, and the bedridden could only thrash and howl until exhaustion took them, which gave it its name: the wailing sickness, or the Wailing.

| Aspect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| When | Before or around the humans' arrival. Which is a deliberate mystery |
| Where it began | Nospier Point, the port city hit first and hardest |
| The danger | Without its fever, the disease cripples the body. The fever itself runs so hot so fast that it can kill |
| The treatment | Keep the patient alive through the fever without suppressing it, with very specific dosing until it runs its course |
| Now | Once the treatment was found, the disease could be kept in check |

The Wailing took a toll on every community, but it left Nospier Point on the brink of collapse.

### The arrival

The humans came from the homeland, driven out by the same disaster that sent the refugees who ended up at Thrallstrand. What that disaster was stays unwritten. Humans were immune to the Wailing, so while the rest of Abernys kept its distance from the source of the outbreak, they had time to establish themselves in a city already full of empty dwellings.

How many generations ago any of this happened is deliberately never stated, but it is recent enough never to feel like ancient history.

## The Covenant

The Covenant isn't a religion so much as a bureaucracy that people live inside. It is the social contract taken to its extreme: everything is contracts and leverage, and nothing exists unless it's on paper.

### How it works

- **A law applies only to those who sign it.** Until a party signs, the law doesn't bind them.
- **Nobody enters the port without signing.** The Pact won't admit anyone who hasn't put their name to its terms.
- **No contract is unfair.** There is no protection against a bad bargain, and no social welfare to fall back on.
- **Everyone has a place, on paper.** The Pact is pragmatic: anyone who signs has a place in it.

### Who runs it

| Office | Role |
| --- | --- |
| The Governor | Leads the colony |
| The High Arbiter | Heads the Arbiters |
| The Arbiters | A monastic order of notaries. In a society this litigious, the people who make and keep the paper hold real influence. They enforce the law alongside the governor's court and the garrison |

### Faith

The nearest thing the Pact has to a faith is the Arbiters themselves and the greater good.

## Indenture, children and tattooed contracts

### Indenture

With no protection for unfair contracts and no welfare, most poorer people are indentured. The Pact doesn't count this as slavery, because every party agreed.

The first indentured beastkin were the survivors of Nospier Point, along with anyone in the area willing to sign a document for food.

### Children

Children aren't exempt. Responsible parents enter a pact with their children that prevents them from making pacts with anyone else, which protects them from being taken advantage of. That protection isn't guaranteed.

### Contracts on the body

The most important contracts are tattooed on the body. The most common are marriage contracts, and punitive indenture for a broken contract.

## Humans and beastkin

Humans and beastkin each see the other as "other", more than either sees the other kinds of its own. How the two adjusted to each other's existence stays offstage.

- Any beastkin can have a place in the Pact by signing, but a legal system foreign to them puts them at a distinct disadvantage.
- Mixed families live mostly outside the walls.
- Humans are neither predator nor prey, so nobody on Abernys knows how to read them *(Region seed)*.
- To the Pact, Abernys is already somewhat alien, and the Woed more so. They keep their distance from it and from the Hushed.

## Border clashes and patrols

There is no active war, only border clashes with Cairn Maw on the downs and with Fenmire in the fen.

- The garrison's patrols survey land, hunt runaways and guard diggers.
- Clashes with Fenmire are common, and the fen won't sell to the colony if it can help it *(Fenmire doc)*.
- The garrison captain holds the colony's soldiers and best weapons *(Societies doc)*.

## Economy and neighbours

Port Covenant is the smaller and more difficult of the coast's two ports, because of its temperamental weather, but it has richer fishing and fewer pirates. It builds ships *(Region seed)* and buys what it can from whoever will sell.

| Neighbour | Stance | Why |

| Fenmire | Hostile | Border clashes, and no fen trade if it can be helped |
| The pirates | Steady trade | Caerwyn is hit harder by pirates and the Pact is short of options, so it trades with them more than most |
