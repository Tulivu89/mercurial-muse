## The three clans

Each clan is one species and closed to the others. Before the first migrations they were the original powers on Abernys and spent their strength on each other. Now they're allies by necessity, and common ground comes easier now that they're forced to share it. *(Owner)*

Their temperaments follow their animals loosely. *(Suggestion)*

### Clan Aurochs

Cattle folk, the biggest people on the downs. Slow to start anything and hard to stop once started. They dug the ditches and raised the great barrows, and they burn chalk into lime in kilns along the scarp, selling it downhill for mortar and fields. They remember every wrong done to the clan, further back than anyone else bothers to. Their downs run south toward Port Covenant.

A head holds Aurochs by being the man nobody will wrestle.

### Clan Hart

Deer folk, proud and watchful. A Hart man's standing is his name: insults get answered, boasts get made good, and a head who's shamed in front of the clan doesn't stay head long. They'll pull back from a fight they can't win and never forgive whoever made them do it. They dig and keep the dew ponds, keep bees on the downland flowers, and walk the high ground as scouts. Their downs face north, toward the jarldoms.

### Clan Crag

Rams and goats, on the steep chalk faces and in the coombes. Stubborn, contrary, sure-footed where others slide. Their families fight among themselves constantly and make it up just as fast. They cut flint from galleries in the chalk, and they'll sell it to anyone.

Crag heads win their place in duels and lose it the same way, so it changes hands often.

## The balance

The tensions pull every way, and while they stay even, nothing moves. At any moment one clan is pushing for a fight and the others are grumbling about it. The clans hate every newcomer, and they know they can't take on all of them at once. *(Owner)*

### Why nobody marches *(Suggestion)*

| Clan | Wants to fight first | Why the others won't go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aurochs | Port Covenant. The patrols cross Aurochs downs | Turning south leaves the wolves at everyone's back |
| Hart | The jarldoms. The wolves took their downs first | Turning north leaves the colony free to push inland |
| Crag | Nobody. They sell flint to the colony and to Caerwyn | Whoever offers Crag the better deal gets their spears, so neither side can count on them |

A new head can flip a clan's stance overnight, and so can one bad week: a patrol kills a herdsman, a jarl's tax party takes a season's honey, a Crag cart is seen at the colony gate.

## The grievance

The clans count themselves displaced and want Abernys returned to them. Beyond that grievance they share little culture and no government. *(Owner)*

Fenmire holds deeper grudges, but the fen folk keep to the fens. The Cairn Maw clans want the coast, the river and the city back. *(Owner)*

## The downs

**From the region seed:** chalk uplands inland, dry high ground the first peoples kept through every conquest. The clans keep the barrows. Colonists want the grave goods, and the keepers kill diggers.

### Land and what it gives *(Suggestion)*

| Feature | What it is | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downland turf | Thin chalk turf thick with thyme and clover, miles of it | Hart beehives |
| Dew ponds | Clay-lined hollows that hold rainwater. The chalk swallows everything else, so these are the only water on the tops | Dug and kept by Hart. Every clan drinks from them, and they're the first thing fought over in a dry summer |
| The scarp | The steep chalk edge above the lowland | Aurochs lime kilns |
| Coombes and cliffs | Dry valleys and white faces cut into the downs | Crag homes, and the flint galleries under them |
| Ditches and banks | Old earthworks from the clan wars, still marking where one clan's downs end | Boundaries nobody agrees on |

Flint mines like these are real: Grime's Graves in Norfolk is a field of Neolithic shafts sunk through chalk to the flint.

### The barrows *(Suggestion)*

Each clan keeps its own barrows, and a barrow marks the clan's claim on the downs around it. Some went up during the clan wars on land that changed hands afterward, and two clans still claim them.

## Law, and the host that hasn't gathered

### Law

Each clan's head judges his own clan. Between clans there is no court, only the heads talking, or not. From the societies doc:

- **Owning people:** forbidden. Slavers caught on the downs don't leave.
- **Predation:** exile or death, whatever the cause.

### The host *(Suggestion)*

In the societies doc, the clans gather a host and choose a war leader when they agree on a war. They haven't agreed on one, so there is no host and no war leader. The border clashes that do happen are fought by whichever clan's downs got crossed.
