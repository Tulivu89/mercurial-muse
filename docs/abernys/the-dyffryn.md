## The earlier migration

**Established** *(Region seed)*

- Foxfolk, boarfolk and bearfolk, all Bitterroot peoples, came in a migration known only from legend. The route is lost.
- They mixed with the first peoples and are settled and prosperous, and distrusted by both sides.
- Open question from the region seed: did they come through the root roads rather than by sea? That would explain why nobody remembers a route.
- The Cairn Maw clans count the valley as first-peoples land farmed by newcomers. *(Cairn Maw doc, suggestion)*

**To decide**

- [ ] What the legend of the land across the sea says, and whether anyone believes it.
- [ ] How mixed the valley is: mixed villages, or separate ones side by side.
- [ ] Whether "distrusted by both sides" means the first peoples and the later waves, or something else.

## Land, tithes and debt

**Established**

- Landholders hold their own land and pay tithes to whoever is strongest that year, which is mostly Caerwyn. *(Societies doc)*
- Caerwyn's great houses lend to the farms. The council's first chair holds the debts of half the valley. *(Societies doc)*
- Bonded labour for debt is legal here. *(Societies doc)*
- The valley's grain is something the city can't do without. *(Societies doc)*

**To decide**

- [ ] Who else has taken tithes in a strong year: the jarldoms, the pirates, Cairn Maw?
- [ ] How the Afon's floods treat the valley, and whether the farms trade by river barge down past the Woed.
- [ ] Whether the valley is one stretch of farms or several valleys, each with its own leading landholder.

## Decisions needed first

The other regions each started from one core idea from the owner: the Hushed cult for the Woed, pride in a hard life for Fenmire, the Covenant for Port Covenant, and the balance for Cairn Maw. The valley needs the same before the rest can be written:

1. **The core idea.** What makes the valley distinct.
2. **What it raises.** A replacement for "cattle".
3. **The earlier migration.** How much of the legend, and the root-road question, the valley carries.
