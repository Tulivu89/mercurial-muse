## What the Woed is

The Woed is a boggy forest along the Afon's middle reaches, joined to the Whispering Woods through roots too deep to trace. One community lives with it: the Hushed, a cult that guides outsiders across it and trades in what it yields, including the only cure for Rabitis. *(Owner unless marked)*

## The peat dead

The Woed's peat preserves bodies, and every one is found the same way: lying in a position of rest with arms crossed over the chest, near the surface, face visible. *(Owner)*

| Aspect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Position | Always the same pose of rest, arms crossed over the chest |
| Depth | Always near the surface, never deep |
| Face | Always visible |
| Touching | Believed to bring a curse *(Belief)* |

Who or what lays them out that way, and why they never sink, is unexplained.

The Hushed treat the peat dead with respect. They leave them where they lie, and sit with them to pray to the whispers. *(Owner)*

## Rabitis and tamecap

Rabitis turns the afflicted entirely feral, and the only known cure is a rare mushroom from the Woed. *(Owner)*

| Aspect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| What it does | Strips every faculty civilization needs. The afflicted can still cooperate with others who have it, stalk prey and mate |
| What's known | Nothing else, except that the mushroom cures it in its early stages |
| The cure | A mushroom found only in the Woed. It can't be cultivated and is never found in large quantities |

The Hushed trade the mushroom, but they aren't the only ones who gather it. *(Owner)* Everything else about the disease stays open, deliberately.

The mushroom is called **tamecap**. *(Owner)*

## The serpent's teaching

Generations ago a serpent came back out of the Woed claiming he had communed with the whispering dead, and his teaching founded the Hushed. *(Owner)*

| What he taught | Status |
| --- | --- |
| The Woed exposes the rot in communities and in each person | Belief. Like any good cult's teaching, it speaks to some truth |
| Only in the Woed are people kept honest with themselves and each other | Belief |
| At the end of all things, all things must give up, so it is an honor to die peacefully in the Woed | Belief. No proof |
| The dead there can be communed with | Belief. Unfounded |
| Dying there preserves the community forever | Belief. Unfounded |

None of it is a supernatural revelation. If there is malice behind or within it, it isn't obvious from the outside.

One overlap with Bitterroot canon is worth keeping in view: the Whispering Woods lures people with the feeling that surrendering is finally a way to rest, and the serpent's teaching calls that surrender an honor. Whether that's coincidence is left open.

## The Hushed

The Hushed are a single communal cult living with the Woed under a leader called the Paterfamilias. They're named for the silence they keep on the Woed, which isn't about hiding: you have to be quiet to listen for the whispers. *(Owner unless marked)*

### Life inside

The community is communal. Nobody is alone and everything is shared. Leaders audit their flock, in the manner of Scientology's auditing, and everyone is expected to share their feelings about each other and about themselves.

There's no obvious ban on leaving, but the structure of a cult makes it difficult.

### On the Woed

- The Hushed maintain the plank bridges. The bog eventually swallows everything, so the upkeep never stops.
- They don't share their maps. They hire themselves out as guides instead.
- Nobody goes anywhere alone, and outsiders don't count as company.
- Children are tied together when they venture out.

### With outsiders

They deal with the outside world, mostly through trade and guiding, but members aren't allowed to socialize with outsiders.

The Hushed and Fenmire don't deal with each other. Each side has its reasons, and neither needs the other's goods. The fen folk burn their dead so no soul can be trapped, and think the Hushed misguided at best, charlatans and conmen at worst. The one exception is a fen witch sending someone for a Woed ingredient. *(Owner)*

### Where they live

Their settlement is called the Fold. *(Owner)*

Proposed site *(Suggestion)*: a rise of firm ground deep in the Woed, reachable only by the plank bridges, so every trader who wants to deal there needs a Hushed guide. The alternative is a site at the Woed's edge on the Afon, where trade comes to them.

## The Paterfamilias and succession

The Paterfamilias is the father to all, since all the Hushed are family. The title passes from father to son, and the handover is often tricky, because leaders tend to have many children among many women. *(Owner)*

| Person | Established | Open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The serpent | The first Paterfamilias. Came out of the Woed generations ago claiming to have communed with the dead, and founded the teaching | Name |
| The current Paterfamilias | Father to all; holds the title by descent | Name, people, age |
| His sons | Many, by many women, so any of them can press a claim | Who's in line, and who wants it |

Whether the line is still serpentfolk depends on whether children can be born across species, which the region seed leaves open.

In Orbis, the line works as a family record: each Paterfamilias and heir in `people`, linked by `parent` relationships, so contested claims show on the tree.
