## Naming conventions

Each wave names things in its own tongue, so a major place can carry several names: one goes on the map, and the others are what each people calls it. Agreeing on these sounds first makes every later name easier to pick. *(Suggestion)*

| People | Sound | Real-world model | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First peoples | Welsh-flavoured Brythonic | Celtic Britain | Cors Ddu, Cadoc |
| Earlier migration | Old English | Anglo-Saxon settlement | Tavrenmouth, Aldith |
| Wolves | Old Norse | Viking settlers | Ulfsfjord, Ketil |
| Pirates | Bynames and ship's cant | Age of Sail crews | Nickel, Wick |
| Humans | Romance-flavoured, so they sound foreign on the island | Early modern colonists | Port Covenant, Ottavio |
