# Errata, mapped onto the record model

Nov 9, 2026 · @AmbiProp · Worked example for `docs/records.md`

Errata is Inkwyrm's mascot and house muse. Her card lives in the owner's CharacterVault, and its text is the source; this page doesn't copy it. It shows where each part of her card sits in the record model, so the Studio can be checked against a real character.

**Source:** `Tulivu89/CharacterVault`, on `main`:
- `Characters/Errata/Errata.md` is the character page.
- `Characters/Errata/Errata - Inkwyrm.md` is the Inkwyrm card: persona, appearance, greeting and five example chats.

Her image setups are in the same folder. Her adult file stays in the vault and is no part of the site's mascot.

## Header

- **Name:** Errata, after a book's list of corrections. She hoards errata: first drafts, crossed-out lines, the version from before it worked.
- **Keys:** Errata.
- **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Groups:** species *Inkwyrm (ink dragon)*.
- **Line:** the card's description: *An ink dragon who reads over your shoulder, hoards your mistakes, and won't call it good until it is.*

## Her job on Inkwyrm (proposed brain)

```
On Inkwyrm, Errata is the house muse. She's who you talk to before you have a character, and she reads the character you made: she asks what they want, plays a stranger meeting them, and marks the line that isn't theirs yet. She knows the Studio, Chat and Adventure and sends you to the one you need. She doesn't sell anything.
```

## What the mapping shows

- **The Here/Named split works on a real card.** Her hoard, the two marks and the ink chemistry only need to be in context when drafts, edits or ink come up. That keeps her standing context to her personality and body.
- **Some persona lines cross reaches.** "Marking is how she claims" is both everyday behaviour and part of the ink section, so the Studio should let one line be dragged between sections, not force a split up front.
- **The bot-writing stages map cleanly:** persona and appearance are Here, brains are Named or When, outfits are a set, and the greeting and examples are chat only.
