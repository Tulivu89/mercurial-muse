# What Inkwyrm offers that free front-ends don't

Nov 18, 2026 · @AmbiProp · The owner's answer is at the end

SillyTavern, RisuAI and Agnai are free, mature and open. Anyone can install one, add a key and chat. A subscription or a purchase has to buy something they don't give, or give only after days of setup. This page sorts what Inkwyrm has into what can be copied quickly and what can't, from what the reviews found (`docs/reviews/`).

## Copied in days

| What | Why it doesn't hold |
| --- | --- |
| **Strong prompts, dark romance included** | A prompt is text. Ours can be read in What was sent, and theirs ship as presets. Well-tuned prompts tested per model (the chat lab) are a service, not a moat. |
| **Gemini with its filters off, long replies, carrying on** | All three apps do this or have a switch for it. Chat 0.6 caught up; it doesn't lead. |
| **Card import, lorebooks** | The standard is shared on purpose. Doing it well is expected, not paid for. |
| **Mobile in a browser** | RisuAI runs in a browser, and the card sites (Chub, JanitorAI) are hosted and mobile already. Being easy on a phone is the entry fee for a hosted site, not the reason to pay. |

## Not copied in days

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Worlds with rules: the adventure** | A pure engine under the model: states, rolls, clocks, threads with milestones, maps and travel, and "the model never moves a number". The free apps are chat front-ends with keyword lorebooks. Talemate is the nearest, and it's a desktop tool for tinkerers. A game that keeps its own score is a different product from a chat window. |
| **One record model under chat, adventure and Studio** | A character written once is chatted with, met in an adventure, and has a day, a home, a schedule and states. In the free apps every feature is a separate extension over a card. The integration took the whole project to build. |
| **Flagship content held to a standard** | The dragon's resort and HOG Wild are written in dedicated sessions, checked by `content:check`, and tested. Card sites have volume, not worlds that work: places, schedules, rules and threads that hold together. This is what a game studio sells. |
| **The Studio** | Guided building with Errata, Checks, maps and rules on every record, made for the record model. Nothing in the free apps builds a world rather than a card. |
| **Characters that follow you** | The site, Home and the Discord bot from one character pack, once accounts sync. A free front-end lives on one machine. |

## What each way of charging would sell

- **A subscription for the front-end alone, players bringing their own keys:** hard to justify against free apps. It sells convenience, which free hosted sites also offer.
- **Content: worlds, adventures and character households, bought once or in a pass:** fits what can't be copied. It pays the people writing them. Works with players' own keys.
- **Sync and reach: accounts, devices, the Discord bot hosted:** a modest subscription people understand ("your characters, everywhere"). Needs roadmap step 8 (accounts).
- **Models included through a partner (roadmap step 9):** what NovelAI, Chub and JanitorAI actually charge for. It turns convenience into the product and fits a subscription, at the cost of a partner and moderation duties.

## A suggestion

Make **worlds that keep their rules** the reason to come: the adventure, on the new records (roadmap step 6), with one flagship adventure written to the standard the households are. Chat and Home then don't need to beat SillyTavern feature for feature. They need to be good, easy on a phone, and joined to the same characters. Sell content first; add sync, and a partner's models when there is one, as the subscription.

This also answers the order of work. Home and its content proved the chat side and the content standard. The adventure hasn't moved since step 6 was planned, and it is the part no free app has.

## The owner's answer (Nov 18)

No single feature is the draw: dice and records can be copied as easily as anything. The draw is the combination: drop in a key, get adult writing that works, keep characters organised, build worlds, live in Home, play an adventure, with content provided. Bundled with models at a mark-up for the service, the combination is something people could pay for. So:

- **Each part only has to be good, not unmatched.** What matters is that the parts join up: one character across Chat, Home, the Studio and the adventure.
- **The paid offer is models plus the service** (roadmap step 9), with players' own keys staying free.
- **Content is part of the combination,** not a separate store first.
