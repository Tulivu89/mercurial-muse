# Brand shortlist

Nov 7, 2026 · @AmbiProp · For the owner's choice

One name for the site, with plain labels for its sides: **Studio** (make characters and worlds), **Adventure** (play a world with rules) and **Chat** (talk with characters). Plain labels never collide with anyone's trademark, and they say what each side does.

## What the name has to do

- Read aloud on first sight, and spell back after hearing it once.
- Cover any genre, not only fantasy.
- Fit what the site is: a place that keeps your characters and worlds, and feeds the right parts of them to the story.
- Carry the owner's taste: dragons and their hoards, dice and rules, pulp-voice storytelling.
- Be free: no live AI chat, story or game product using it.

## Candidates

Screened by web search on Nov 7, 2026. Domains could not be checked from here; check each at a registrar before deciding.

| Name | Why | Screening |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Talehoard** (recommended) | A dragon's hoard of stories. The studio is literally your hoard, and every story draws from it. One word, two syllables, no wrong way to say it. Works for a romance chat as well as a dungeon. | No product found under the name. Nearby "Tale" apps (TaleTell, Talelet, TaleCloud) are children's story apps, far enough away. |
| **Storyhoard** | The same idea, plainer. | No product found. Reads close to "storyboard", which could cost search traffic. |
| **Mercurial Muse** | The working name: changeable and inspiring. | No product found, but "Muse" is crowded in AI (Microsoft's Muse model, several Muse apps). Two words and three syllables in the first. |
| **Inkwyrm** | An ink dragon: writing with teeth. | Only an itch.io user named Inkwyrmling. "Wyrm" trips readers who don't play fantasy games. |
| **Wyrmtale** | A dragon's tale. | No product found. Same "wyrm" problem, and close to World of Warcraft's "Wyrmtail". |

**Avoided:** anything starting with "Lore". The field is full: LoreKeeper (an AI game master), LoreKeep (a story-bible app), Loreweaver, LoreBrain, Lore Machine.

## With Talehoard

- Talehoard Studio, Talehoard Adventure, Talehoard Chat in full; Studio, Adventure and Chat inside the site.
- A line for the front page: *Keep every character. Play every story.*
- Domains to check, in order: talehoard.com, talehoard.app, talehoard.gg.
- The mark: a coiled dragon around a book or a stack of cards. The look can start from the adventure's (dark ground, Literata for story text, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for the interface).

## After the choice

1. Register the domain and point it at the server, which replaces the sslip.io address.
2. Put the name in each side's display text (roadmap step 3).
3. Check the name against the trademark registers where the site will be marketed.
