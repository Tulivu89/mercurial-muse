Map prototype

Bogwater's 38 places, laid out from what holds what. Nothing here was placed by hand, and no route is needed to travel. Times come from the levels and the map. Move places on the ground, or change what one cell is worth, and watch the times change.

From where you stand

    Travel by level

    A level is what a place is: a land, a region, a settlement, a building. Each has a typical trip across it and a cost to step inside one of its places. One cell on a map is a quarter of a typical trip, until a sheet sets its own.

    Try the other ways

    What to look for

    • Circles and ground are one layout. The circles are each sheet of hexes, rounded. Move a place on the ground and its circle moves too.
    • A journey climbs, crosses and comes down. It goes up to the level where both places sit side by side, across that sheet's map, then down again. Tap a place to see each leg.
    • Scale is one number per sheet. Change what one cell is worth on a sheet, and every trip across it scales with it.
    • Adding is free. Add a place on the ground and it finds room. On an arranged sheet nothing else moves.
