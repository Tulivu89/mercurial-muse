# The look and layout: the plan

Nov 12, 2026 · @AmbiProp · Claude's informed proposal, after reading the three bids and its own. The owner asked for it to be built in the same pass, so each phase below says what shipped. Anything here is still open to veto.

The brief is `brief.md`; Claude's own proposal, written before any bid was read, is `claude.md`; the bids are `chatgpt.md`, `gemini-3-8-flash.md` and `grok.md` (its pictures are `mockups.html` and `frames/`). ChatGPT says it read `claude.md` before writing, so where the two agree it counts once, not twice. Grok's bid came as a zip of its whole workspace; its own files were taken out of it and the zip removed, and it is still in the history.

Pictures of what was built, to set beside the brief's `screens/`, are in `built/`.

## The plan in five lines

1. **A record's page is the writing.** The other jobs are doors: one row each for the day, the rules, the adventure, chat and In context now, each with a sentence that says what is set. A door fills a phone; on a wide screen it opens beside the writing.
2. **The list goes one level at a time:** the world, then a kind, then inside a place. Every row carries the record's line. Find crosses all of it.
3. **Back works.** Every page has an address, the list keeps its place, and a detour to In context now returns to the same word.
4. **World › Rules is a list of its parts,** each saying what it has changed, with a filter for only what's changed.
5. **One header for the site and the adventure in Wet Ink,** with its layout untouched: no header in play, the menu under the prompt.

## Where they agree, so this takes it as settled

All four:
- keep the Studio as list, page and context side by side when there is room, and one thing at a time on a phone;
- break up the record page by job, with the writing first and no author mode that hides parts of a world;
- give World › Rules an index of its parts and a filter for what is changed;
- restyle the adventure and move nothing: one scroll, the story, the prompt, the five sections, a check in the prompt's place, no header in play;
- put one header on the Studio, Chat and the adventure's start screen, with the model key set once for all three;
- add no library.

## The Studio, as built

**The frame.**

| Width | What shows |
| --- | --- |
| Under 900 px | One thing at a time: the list, then a record, then a door. Each has a way back in words. |
| 900 to 1199 | The list and the page. A door takes the page's place. |
| 1200 and up | The list, the page, and one door at the right: In context now until another is opened. |

**The list.**
- **The world's page:** Find, the records opened lately, each kind with its count, then About, Rules, Checks and In context now, each with its state in words (*42 changed*, *All clear*, *356 tokens*).
- **A kind:** its records, A to Z or as added, each with its line.
- **Places:** the top places first. A place's name opens its page; *3 inside* goes into it. Inside, the trail (*Abernys › Fenmire*) leads back out, and Add puts the new place there.
- **Find** gives one flat list: the name, the kind, where it is, and the key or the words of the line that matched. Clearing it puts the list back where it was.

**A record's page.** The name, the kind, and one line of identity (*The witch · she/her · Harefolk*). **Names and keys** folds the name, keys, line, pronouns, gender and groups; it is open on a new record. Then the doors, then Sections, Sets, Notes, and Delete as a word.

| Door | What is behind it | On |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Day** | Where they live, the week, and for each event where it is and what it waits on | Characters |
| **Rules** | What a player can do here and how its states read | Characters, personas, places, items, groups |
| **In the adventure** | Where a thing lies, a place's routes, a thread's milestones, where play starts | Items, places, threads, scenarios |
| **Chat** | Greetings and example chats | Characters |
| **In context now** | The moment and what it sends | Every record, below 1200 px |

**World › Rules.** Ten parts as rows (approaches, states, tiers, dice and rest, deeds, growth, trade and making, goods, the calendar, scene actions), each reading *standard* or how many it has changed. A part opens alone. **Only what's changed** narrows the list to the parts that differ and each part to its changed lines.

## The adventure, as built

The layout is as it was. What changed:
- **Paper and Night** from the site's tokens, with the switch in Settings beside text size.
- **Type:** Fraunces for the menu's headings and the start screen, Literata for the story, Atkinson for the rest. The second font for numbers is gone; figures line up by the font's own setting.
- **The story:** narration in italic grey ink, speech plain. The adventure already marked speech, so nothing had to be guessed. Your own turns sit in blue-black.
- **The prompt:** actions wrap instead of scrolling off the side (ChatGPT, Grok). Send is the seal and the only one.
- **Shapes:** 6 px controls, 10 px cards, 4 px tags. No pill-shaped buttons.
- **The approach and dice colours stay,** darkened for paper. They carry the rules' meaning, as the marks carry the brand's.
- **The start screen** takes the shared header. In play there is none; Settings ends with the ways to Chat and the Studio.

## One site, as built

- **The header** (`kit/src/SiteHeader.tsx`): the side's own mark, the wordmark when there is room, **Adventure · Chat · Studio** with the current one underlined in seal, and a gear.
- **Settings behind the gear on every side:** the look and the model key. In the Studio it is a sheet, in Chat its Settings page, and on the adventure's start screen the last part of the page, as it is the last section in play. The look and the key were already stored once for all three.
- **Crossings:** *Chat with* on a character and *Play in the adventure* on a world, as before. New: a character in Chat that came from a world has **Edit in the Studio**, which opens that record. From the adventure back to a record waits until a save remembers which world it came from (ChatGPT's caution).

## One thing a bid found that isn't layout

Gemini warned that 800 records could outgrow the browser's 5 MB of plain storage. Worlds are safe: they have been in IndexedDB since 0.1.1. But **Play in the adventure** hands a world over through that small store, and keeps the last six. A big world could fail to launch. It is a small fix (hand over one world at a time, or through IndexedDB) and is not in this pass.
