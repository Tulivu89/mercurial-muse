# Volungraiv — gold records

## 1. The Lee

**Line** · *Named; lists*  
The Lee is Hollis Crane's caravanserai inside Qetzal's Gate, where glass Peace tokens knock together beneath rooms overlooking the wagon yard.

**Entry** · *Here*  
Inside the Gate, the Lee holds wagons and beasts against a titan rib, polished by passing hubs. Rented rooms ring a gallery above the common room; Room Six faces the yard. Hollis Crane keeps the keys and pours after dusk. Porters work the teams and carry unclaimed dead to the Marrow's Mouth.

**Atmosphere** · *Here*  
**Morning:** Porters strain upper-city runoff at the trough. **Afternoon:** Hot harness leather and dung sour the yard. **Evening:** Koriar's band carries down from the high bone; Peace tokens tap against cups. **Night:** Lanterns smoke over the beasts; Ansel sings tavern songs flat.

**History** · *Named; keywords: Ivo, Room Six, buy-out, porters, Marrow's Mouth*  
Ivo, a harper who fled Gildra's palace, was knifed in Room Six last night. He still wore his Peace. Wenna had inked its buy-out; the underguilds stood down. At dawn the Lee's porters scrubbed the gallery boards and took his body to the Marrow's Mouth.

**When the Gate is shut** · *When: Gate shut*  
When the bell sounds, porters drive wagons against the rib, clearing space for arrivals. Once the Gate shuts, the Camps stay outside. The common room fills onto the stairs, and Hollis sells floor space. In a glass storm, guests pull their bedding away from the upper windows.

## 2. Tamsin

**Line** · *Named; lists*  
Tamsin is a Solarkin knife lodging at the Lee, with unmarked skin and a price for names the Counting-House has inked.

**Appearance** · *Here; 332 characters*  
Tamsin has bronze Solarkin skin, thick calves, narrow hips and russet hair cut to the nape. She stands with her weight on her heels; when seated, she props a knee against the table edge. When glass strikes stone, her left fingers close before she turns. No ink marks her face, throat or arms. One upper tooth ends in a square break.

**Personality** · *Here; 351 characters*  
Ask about a killing and she asks who inked the buy-out. Offer her coin for a protected target and she returns it. Lie about a witness and she goes to find that witness. Threaten her and she calls for someone to witness the threat. Help her and she insists you sit beside her in the Lee's open yard, where others can see. She never asks a buyer's name.

**Speech** · *Here; 371 characters*  
Her voice is low and grainy, with the coast's vowels drawn long. Questions come in short pieces; when someone gets the Peace wrong, her answers run to full sentences. She says 'holding' for a live Peace and 'inked' for a buy-out; 'murder' means a killing outside the law. After drink she joins refrains a beat late. Under threat she uses full names, without contractions.

**Background** · *Named; keywords: Solarkin, coast, Lightbringers, glyphs, judges*  
She left the Lightbringers' coast young, before a judge inked anything on her. Solarkin there bear the crimes and oaths that priests write into their skin. In Qetzal, she has killed under bought-out Peace and remained unmarked. Travelers from the coast sometimes ask to inspect her wrists.

**Wants** · *Named; keywords: Ivo, payment, proxy, Gildra's gold, Ansel, Sela*  
The proxy who hired Tamsin to knife Ivo has left her unpaid. The first time he hired her, he gave her foreign gold; Sela changed it at Koriar's table, where Tamsin saw Gildra's face stamped on the coin. She can demand her fee from Ansel only by naming his money aloud. She suspects everyone who can identify the payer will appear in the next buy-outs.

**One hook — Caravan stars** · *Named; keywords: stars, night sky, caravan stars, navigation*  
When the sky clears above the Gate, she names the caravan stars for anyone beside her. She points out which the titan's ribs block. When dust hides them all, she draws their paths in the drink pooled under her cup.

**When: after the count** · *When: buy-out count known*  
She stops placing Pit bets. Between bouts she finds Teague and asks which names his crew was ordered to leave alone. At dusk she calls on Wenna for the inked names and waits while Wenna decides whether to show her.

**When: once the coin is known** · *When: someone else identifies Gildra's coin*  
She asks the second person who knows the coin to say Gildra's name where Hollis can hear. She takes her evening drink at the gate-facing end of the yard and waits for Ansel to come through.

**When: once Ansel's Peace is bought out** · *When: Ansel's name is inked*  
She has Wenna show her the ink, then demands Ivo's unpaid fee directly from Ansel in the Lee's yard. She accepts offers against him now. She asks prospective buyers to come while Ansel can hear them.

## 3. Why it's gold

- Entry supplies the physical arrangement; each time of day adds a different action or sound.
- The titan rib is a wall a wagon can strike, a place the porters can move beasts against, and shelter when the bell rings.
- Ivo's death leaves the porters to question, Wenna to confront, and Hollis to bargain with.
- Tamsin's unmarked skin tells the coast's law through what another person can see and ask about.
- Her rule can be tested in conversation: she returns money for a target whose Peace still holds.
- The caravan stars give her a conversation with a stranger that needs no murder, payment, or quest.
- Each When section changes whom she approaches and where she risks being seen.

## 4. Inventions beyond the brief

- **The Lee:** A titan rib forms a side of the wagon yard and is polished by wagon hubs; Room Six faces the yard; Hollis keeps the room keys; a trough takes runoff from the upper city; the yard has harnesses, smoking lanterns and upper windows; guests' Peace tokens tap against cups; porters scrubbed the gallery boards after Ivo's death; Hollis charges for floor space when the Gate shuts.
- **Tamsin's body and manner:** Bronze skin, thick calves, narrow hips, russet hair cut to the nape, a heel-weighted stance, a knee against the table, a reflex when glass strikes stone, and a square break in an upper tooth.
- **Tamsin's speech and habits:** A grainy low voice, lengthened coastal vowels, the terms *holding* and *inked*, her distinction between murder and a legal killing, late singing, full names under threat, seeking witnesses, and being seen publicly with anyone who helps her.
- **Tamsin's past and aside:** Travelers from the coast ask to see her wrists; she points out stars concealed by the titan's ribs and traces star paths in spilled drink.
- **Thread responses:** She questions Teague and Wenna after the count, asks a second witness to identify Gildra's coin within Hollis's hearing, waits publicly for Ansel, and invites offers against him once his name is inked.
- **Rules:** All difficulty, rank, approach, alert and purse ratings; her hidden knife and leather jerkin.
