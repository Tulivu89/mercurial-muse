# Volungraiv Gold Pair: the Lee & Tamsin

---

## Record 1: the Lee

**Line** *(Named, lists)*
A walled caravanserai mortared against Qetzal's lowest titan rib, where Old Road crews stable beasts, rent gallery rooms, and wait out glass storms.

**Entry** *(Here)*
A stone beast yard fronts a common room and an upper gallery, Room Six at the bend. Hollis Crane keeps it and pours from evening on. Porters tend the gate, shovel storm-glass, and haul unclaimed dead to the Marrow drain.

**Atmosphere** *(Here)*
Morning: grey light off the rib, city runoff rushing the gutters, lye and mule dung, harness chains. Afternoon: white glare on yard grit, flies at the troughs, distant Pit shouts. Evening: oil lamps, mutton fat, Koriar's band carrying down, Ansel singing flat. Night: barred gate, low wicks, damp stone, creaking gallery planks.

*(Entry + Atmosphere: 92 words / ~120 tokens)*

**History** *(Named — keywords: `the Lee`, `Room Six`, `Hollis`, `porters`, `Marrow`)*
Hollis leased the Lee from the Compact and rents beds to anyone with coin. After a buy-out killing, porters clear the room before dawn and drop unclaimed dead down the Marrow drain. They scrubbed Room Six at first light after Ivo died; stain still holds in the boards.

**When the Gate is shut** *(When)*
After the gate bell rings, iron bars drop on the arch and everyone who beat the Gate packs the Lee; stragglers stay out in the Camps. Porters lash hide tarps over beasts while glass hail batters the roof or a behemoth thuds past the rib. Hollis doubles chalked rates and sells floor space until the Gate unbars.

## Record 2: Tamsin

**Line** *(Named, lists)*
A Solarkin buy-out knife lodging at the Lee, her pale gold hands, forearms, and throat bare of any ink.

**Appearance** *(Here — 405 / 420 characters)*
Short and wiry, with pale gold skin, cropped red hair, and sun-lined grey eyes. She walks straight through crowds in flat, even strides, arms loose. At rest, she leans back with her chin tipped skyward and hands laid palm-up on her knees. When someone reaches for her arm, a red flush climbs her throat and her shoulder jerks back. From knuckles to collarbone, her skin holds faint blade-nicks and no ink.

**Personality** *(Here — 404 / 420 characters)*
Asked for work, she checks Counting-House ink first; bribed to kill an unbought Peace or drop a job, she pushes the coin back. Threatened, she calls out her Peace for witnesses and orders another cup. Lied to, she stops answering, settles her tab, and leaves. Helped unasked, she pays back equal coin at once. In talk, she presses for Pit odds, road prices, and debts, and refuses questions about buyers.

**Speech** *(Here — 398 / 420 characters)*
Low, dry rasp with clipped coast consonants. She speaks in short, flat sentences of plain market talk, stopping when a point ends. She uses Counting-House terms and road weights, drops Lightbringer oaths and polite words for killing, and repeats any name or sum aloud before agreeing. Drink slows her pace into long runs of star names; cornered, her vowels tighten and replies drop to single words.

**Background** *(Named — keywords: `coast`, `Lightbringers`, `ink`, `glyphs`, `priests`)*
She grew up on the Lightbringers' coast, where Solarkin judges ink crimes onto skin on the spot, and walked out young with a caravan before a needle touched her. In Qetzal, buy-out work breaks no law, leaving her skin bare. She lodges at the Lee by Hollis's leave; Teague knows she killed Ivo and cannot touch her.

**Wants** *(Named — keywords: `Ivo`, `Ansel`, `gold`, `proxy`, `buy-out`)*
She wants her pay for Ivo; the proxy who hired her has not paid. Earlier he paid in foreign gold; changing it at Koriar's table showed her the Witch Queen's face on it. Collecting means going to Ansel and naming the gold aloud, when people who know become the next inked names. She keeps her round: barred room mornings, Pit bets afternoons, yard drinks evenings, common room nights.

**One hook** *(Named — keywords: `stars`, `sky`, `Old Road`, `caravan`)*
She knows the stars Old Road caravans steer by. After dusk she points out the Salt-Cart and the Glass-Eye for anyone beside her, traces their bearings in spilled drink, and names the Badlands wells under each.

**When: After the count** *(When)*
She abandons the Lee's open yard and drinks from the upper gallery rail overlooking the gate. Each morning she checks the Counting-House board for her own name before eating. She stops betting at the Pit and turns down every buy-out job brought by a proxy.

**When: Once the coin is known** *(When)*
With anyone who knows the gold, she stops cutting off questions about buyers and names the proxy, Sela's table, and Ansel. She keeps her pack rolled beside her bed, wedges the door at night, and pushes them to fund a buy-out on Ansel before he buys hers.

**When: Once Ansel's Peace is bought out** *(When)*
She goes straight to the Counting-House to read Ansel's buy-out ink herself. Confirmed, she stops drinking, waives her fee, and sets out for him at once, starting at his Lee room. If she kills him, she takes only Ivo's owed sum from his purse.

## Why it's gold

1. **Zero interchangeable lines:** Every sensory cue and habit belongs to a titan-rib desert city where safety is bought and sold—runoff gutters, glass hail, Peace witnesses, Counting-House ink, and bare Solarkin forearms.
2. **One job per field:** Bare skin appears as a visual hook in Line, a physical surface in Appearance, and a coastal fact in Background, with no repeated sentences or leaked explanations.
3. **Acts replace adjectives and feelings:** A red flush and shoulder jerk replace "wary," paying back equal coin replaces "proud," and calling out her Peace replaces "bold," giving weak models concrete beats to narrate.
4. **Playable with zero plot running:** Lye smell in Room Six, Hollis's doubled slate, Ansel singing flat, and star bearings traced in spilled drink let the narrator open a scene at any bench or door.
5. **Clean state deltas in When sections:** Each When block flips one specific habit from Here (open yard to gallery rail, refusing buyer talk to naming Sela's table, waiting on coin to hunting Ansel) instead of rewriting Personality.
6. **Character expressed through constraint:** Tamsin's ruleset never needs to be lectured—it shows up when she pushes back a bribe, checks the ink before moving on Ansel, and takes only Ivo's owed sum from his purse.
