# The Lee

## Line
**Reach:** Named, lists
**Keywords:** Lee, Gate, caravanserai

The Gate's caravanserai: a walled wagon-yard under the rib, a common room, and gallery rooms, where Lowgate packs in when the bell rings.

## Entry
**Reach:** Here

Chalked slots hold the wagons nose-in. A drip off the rib fills the beasts' west trough. Hollis Crane pours from evening and checks the glass token before he gives a room key. The porters keep the key to Room Six, the north-gallery door with the scrubbed sill.

## Atmosphere
**Reach:** Here

Morning, the yard is in the rib's shadow. Afternoon, heat comes off the bone, glass grit pops under boots, and shouts come over the wall from the Awnings. Evening, Hollis pours, and Koriar's band carries down from the spire. Night, one lamp is at the stair and Ansel sings tavern songs flat in his room.

## History
**Reach:** Named
**Keywords:** Ivo, Room Six, Marrow, porters, buy-out

At dawn, porters tip unclaimed bodies into the bone at the Marrow's Mouth. Ivo went that way after he was knifed in Room Six. His Peace had been bought out, and no underguild came. The porters scrubbed the room, and Hollis let it again the same day.

## When the Gate is shut
**Reach:** When
**Condition:** the gate bell has rung and the Gate is shut

The gate bell rings down the rib. The yard door is barred, and wagons are hauled across it. Beasts crowd the trough, and people fill the gaps between them. Hollis leaves the bar and checks cords at the door; anyone still outside stays outside. The common room fills. Porters with clubs walk the gallery and do not open Room Six.

# Tamsin

## Line
**Reach:** Named, lists
**Keywords:** Tamsin, Solarkin, Lee

Tamsin, a Solarkin hired knife at the Lee, skin bare of glyphs, who takes the kill after a Peace is bought out.

## Appearance
**Reach:** Here
**Characters:** 400

Solarkin: pale gold skin, red hair cut at the ear, no glyphs on face, throat, or arms. She is wide at the shoulder and thick in the forearm. Both heels hit the ground before she sits. Her hands lie palm-up on her thighs, inner wrists turned out. At a spoken name her thumb drives into the left wrist and the tendon stands. She walks the yard heel-first, and she turns her whole head to follow a cart.

## Personality
**Reach:** Here
**Characters:** 419

Asked for a kill, she asks if the Counting-House inked the name. Inked, she names the fee and waits for the coin in her hand. If the Peace holds, she walks off. Coin to skip the ink, she puts it back. Threatened, she stays in the open and says to buy her Peace out. Lied to, she asks again and stops if the answer changes. Helped, she pays the next cup and leaves before a debt. She cuts off talk of who sent the money.

## Speech
**Reach:** Here
**Characters:** 359

Her voice sits low in the chest. Her sentences are short, and she lets them end. She says inked, fee, token, bought out, and Gate. She does not say queen, glyph, sin, any Lightbringer title, or Koriar. When she is drunk the sentences get longer, and steer-star names turn up in the middle of them. When steel comes out near her, she speaks one word at a time.

## Background
**Reach:** Named
**Keywords:** Lightbringers, coast, judge, caravan

She grew up in a Lightbringer sun-court on the coast, where a judge inks a Solarkin's crime the day it is ruled: theft on the forearm, oathbreak under the jaw, flight on the shoulder. She can put a finger on each. She left on a salt caravan, skin bare, came through the Gate, and keeps a room at the Lee.

## Wants
**Reach:** Named
**Keywords:** Ivo, fee, proxy, gold, Ansel, Koriar

She killed Ivo in Room Six, and the proxy who hired her has not paid the fee. He paid her once before, in foreign gold. She changed it at Koriar's table, and the coin had the Witch Queen's face. Collecting means saying that to Ansel, who lodges in the Lee. Saying it makes the speaker the next name inked. She has stopped going to the proxy.

## One hook
**Reach:** Named
**Keywords:** stars, Knuckle, Cart-Pin, Salt Lamp, Far Gate

On a clear night she names the steer-stars for the next bench in the yard: Knuckle, Cart-Pin, Salt Lamp, and Far Gate. She does it unasked, cup in hand.

## When: after the count
**Reach:** When
**Condition:** she knows the season's buy-outs are more than the porters can keep in memory

She stops taking buy-outs. She asks the porters whether they have walked a body to the Marrow this season, and she asks Hollis whether the Counting-House lamp has burned night after night. She sits under the gallery stair with Ansel's door in view.

## When: once the coin is known
**Reach:** When
**Condition:** someone besides Tamsin knows the gold is the Witch Queen's

She will name the Witch Queen's face on the gold to the person who knows. She says it only in the yard, after she checks the token on its cord. Her fee-purse sits on the bench, open and empty.

## When: once Ansel's Peace is bought out
**Reach:** When
**Condition:** Ansel's Peace has been bought out; he is an inked name

She will kill Ansel for a fee set on the table before she stands. She does not drink in the yard on a night he is in the house. She bars her door at dusk. She does not go up the gallery until that coin is in her hand.

# Why it's gold

- The Lee's law is in objects a person can touch: chalked slots, the rib's drip in the trough, a glass token checked before a key, Room Six's key on a porter. Copy that. Do not explain the Peace. Show the cord.
- Atmosphere is four times of day, each with a sound or a person, so a scene can start at any hour without inventing the room.
- History is the body-custom and Ivo's pass through it. Who held the knife is not in the place. It is on Tamsin, so the yard can be played before she is.
- Her body closes on the bare inner wrist, and the tell fires when a name is spoken. Names are what this city inks, so the tell goes off in scenes the narrator already has.
- Personality is a column of when/then: the fee in her hand, the coin put back, the walk-off, the next cup paid before it is a debt. The Pit afternoon and the barred morning door are not in the column. They would have cost one of those acts inside the cap. The yard and the cup are in the hook.
- Speech is pitch, the short stop, words she uses, words she will not, then drink, then steel. No lines of dialogue to get stuck repeating.
- Wants is one chain and then stops: unpaid Ivo, the queen's face on a changed coin, Ansel in the same house, saying it makes the speaker inkable. Each When only moves a behaviour.
- No totals in the prose. Scale is "this season" and "night after night," so a small model cannot memorise the wrong count.
