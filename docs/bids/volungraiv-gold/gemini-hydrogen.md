## Record 1: The Lee

**Line** *(Reach: Named, lists | Keywords: `the Lee`, `caravanserai`, `Lowgate`)*
A walled caravanserai inside Qetzal's Gate, where Old Road wagons pack the yard and Hollis Crane pours drinks beneath a dead titan's ribs.

**Entry** *(Reach: Here)*
A walled wagon yard with troughs opens into a common room under a gallery of numbered doors, Room Six among them. Porters haul freight and carry unclaimed dead to the Marrow drain. Hollis Crane keeps the house and pours at the bar from evening on.

**Atmosphere** *(Reach: Here)*
Morning brings runoff down the yard, broom scrapes, and boiled grain. Afternoon dries the cobbles under white glare as harness clanks and dung steams. At dusk, tallow lamps burn over frying fat while Koriar's temple band carries down. At night, knucklebones rattle while Ansel sings tavern songs flat.

**History** *(Reach: Named | Keywords: `the Lee`, `Room Six`, `Marrow drain`, `Compact`, `Ivo`)*
The Compact built the Lee as a storm-proof mule-pound when the Gate rose. Its thick walls still shelter caravans from glass hail. Unclaimed dead go down the Marrow drain at dawn; porters carried a knifed harper out of Room Six this morning with his Peace bought out.

**When the Gate is shut** *(Reach: When)*
Porters bar the yard gate and bolt shutters across the gallery. Glass hail or behemoth tread shakes the rafters while caravans and stragglers pack the yard and benches tight. Hollis cuts off credit and posts a porter at the stair. Anyone outside stays in the Camps until the gate bell rings clear.

## Record 2: Tamsin

**Line** *(Reach: Named, lists | Keywords: `Tamsin`, `Solarkin`, `buy-out`)*
A Solarkin buy-out knife who lodges at the Lee, her bronze skin bare of glyphs or crime marks.

**Appearance** *(Reach: Here | 352 characters)*
Lean and short-waisted, with bronze Solarkin skin and cropped red hair darkened by dust. She walks straight-backed in short steps and sits facing the room, both heels hooked behind her bench legs. When a stranger approaches from her left, the tendon along her collarbone jumps. Her forearms and knuckles stay bare of glyphs, crossed by pale work scars.

**Personality** *(Reach: Here | 380 characters)*
Asked for news or work, she trades fact for fact and requires Counting-House ink on any target. Bribed against an uninked Peace, she pushes the coin back and leaves. Threatened, she names the fee to buy out her own Peace. Lied to, she ends the talk and changes benches. Helped, she pays back the exact turn in silver or watch-hours. In conversation, she presses for who pays whom.

**Speech** *(Reach: Here | 367 characters)*
Low and dry, pitched for the person beside her. She uses short, flat sentences with pauses between them. She keeps to road and Counting-House words for work and coin, avoiding Lightbringer titles and prayers. She repeats a stranger's name back before answering. Drink lengthens her sentences when she names stars; fear strips her speech to single words and coin sums.

**Background** *(Reach: Named | Keywords: `Lightbringers`, `coast`, `glyphs`, `ink`, `bare skin`)*
She grew up on the Lightbringers' coast, where Solarkin judges ink crimes onto skin on the court floor. She fled west on an Old Road wagon before any priest marked her. In Qetzal she took buy-out jobs from the Counting-House and moved into a room at the Lee.

**Wants** *(Reach: Named | Keywords: `Ivo`, `coin`, `proxy`, `Witch Queen`, `Ansel`)*
She wants the coin owed her for killing Ivo. The proxy who hired her has not paid. On an earlier job he paid in foreign gold; Sela changed it at Koriar's table, and Tamsin saw the Witch Queen's face on it. Collecting means going over his head to Ansel and saying aloud whose gold funds the buy-outs, when people who know become the next names in the Counting-House book.

**One hook** *(Reach: Named | Keywords: `stars`, `caravans`, `Old Road`, `night sky`)*
She knows the stars caravans steer by. Sit near her in the yard or common room and she points them out past the titan's ribs, naming storm-season risers and the star that holds wagons on the Old Road.

**When: after the count** *(Reach: When)*
She keeps her door barred past morning, skips her afternoon Pit bets, and stops drinking openly in the yard at dusk. She turns down new Counting-House jobs until Ivo's score is paid, and asks anyone arriving from the Counting-House whose names were inked last.

**When: once the coin is known** *(Reach: When)*
She moves her gear out of her upper room and sleeps behind stacked freight in the yard. She stops hiding what she saw at Koriar's table and offers to identify Ansel's proxy for anyone who helps collect her pay or clears her way out the Gate.

**When: once Ansel's Peace is bought out** *(Reach: When)*
She goes straight to the Counting-House to take Ansel's inked name herself, then waits at the foot of the Lee's gallery stair with her knife ready. She asks only that someone keep Hollis's porters occupied while she climbs to collect Ivo's pay from Ansel's purse.

## Why it's gold

1. **Strict job separation per field:** Appearance holds only body, kin, movement, and a tell (zero clothes or motives); Personality holds only stimulus-response acts (zero history or adjectives); Background holds facts without explaining why she behaves as she does.
2. **Every Here sentence is immediately playable:** The narrator can put a scene straight onto the Lee's troughs, tallow-lit benches, or Room Six stair, or trigger Tamsin's left-side collarbone tell, star-naming, or Counting-House demand without waiting for plot.
3. **World rules live inside ordinary details:** Upper-city runoff, glass hail, the Marrow drain, Koriar's knucklebones, Peace cords, and Solarkin skin free of Lightbringer ink establish Qetzal without exposition.
4. **Thread momentum in the When blocks:** None of the three When sections repeat Personality; each moves Tamsin's physical position in the Lee and shifts what she offers the player.
5. **Model-friendly syntax:** Plain verbs, short declarative clauses, zero metaphors or hedging, and zero unnecessary counts keep weaker 28k-token narrators anchored to concrete action.
6. **Rules tied to fiction:** Every difficulty, rating, item, and her empty purse derives directly from her legal buy-out trade, floor lethality, and unpaid Ivo contract.
