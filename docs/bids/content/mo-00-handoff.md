## What Mythic Overdrive is

An original superhero setting that borrows from popular teams and characters: a sandbox for playing with favourite characters adapted into one world. Anyone can jump in anywhere. Every character is built on a named source so it starts from a proven foundation instead of being invented from scratch.

## Author decisions (canon)

1. **Sandbox.** No fixed protagonist and no main crew.
2. **Powers are re-skinned on purpose**, to keep the setting original. Never restore a source's power; carry personality, relationships and role instead.
3. **Every character has a named source**, listed in the roster below.
4. **The standing memory is bespoke**, not bound to any template's sections. It carries no place descriptions (the gazetteer covers those) and no crew list (the factions list covers that).
5. **Schedules, homes and starting places are the author's** and stay as written.
6. **Ages set:** Cherry Bomb 23, Ice Queen 24, Firefox 18, Ultraviolet 18. All other ages are blank and are the author's to set.

## The main finding

The source lines are better than the drafts made from them. Every Core came out as a creed ("Somebody has to…", "Whatever is out there gets understood…") instead of behaviour, while the source lines are full of behaviour. So the rewrite method is:

1. Draft each Core from its source line, not by revising the existing Core.
2. Check the result against the cast-wide traps in the style file.

## Concepts in progress: Blacklight's Vigilantes

Concepts only, no fields. Homes, starting places and schedules are the author's.

**Blacklight** (Batman, of *Tower of Babel*)
- **Job:** runs Blacklight Industries from the Spires by day, and the Map Table and the Neon on foot by night.
- **Want:** every dangerous thing in Hargrave accounted for, with a way to stop each one, including the people he trains.
- **Pattern:** ruleset, kept. No metas; the rule that breaks is his. That lines up with Abaddon's want in their pair.
- **Aside:** driving alone with no destination, moved from tastes to off duty.
- **Pairs:** Ultraviolet, Abaddon and Cherry exist; Cherry's still needs its "together" line. Umbra and Eclipse are proposed.

**Umbra** (Nightwing)
- **Job:** field leader. He runs the night from the Darkroom and trains on the Range.
- **Want:** the family run on trust instead of orders, and to be the one who made it that way.
- **Pattern:** mask, kept. As Blacklight's lieutenant he carries orders he stopped believing.
- **Aside:** drinking in the Neon with strangers, kept.
- **Pairs:** Eclipse and Ultraviolet exist. A Blacklight ↔ Umbra pair is proposed, and it replaces his "for whom" line.

**Eclipse** (Red Hood)
- **Job:** enforcer, working the Sump alone.
- **Want:** the people who hurt kids put down for good, and Blacklight made to admit that's what the job takes.
- **Pattern:** appetite, kept. Once it starts he can't stop it early; his line is children.
- **Aside:** off duty, reading old novels in the bunks, the longer the better. This replaces his bottle-smelling knack, which is a quirk.
- **Pairs:** Umbra exists. A Blacklight ↔ Eclipse pair is proposed: Blacklight wants Abaddon alive, Eclipse wants him dead, which is Jason and Bruce over the Joker.
- **Drift fixed:** loud again, not near-silent.

**Ultraviolet** (Cassandra Cain)
- **Job:** intel specialist, mapping the Undercity from the Map Table.
- **Want:** the survey as hers, so she can walk out with it.
- **Pattern:** defense, kept. She hides that she stopped believing the purity line.
- **Body-reading** returns as a skill, not a power: she answers what someone's hands did rather than what they said. It's one concrete habit, not "notices everything."
- **Aside:** cheap noodles and serialised trash stay as tastes.
- **Pairs:** Blacklight and Umbra exist.
