# Mythic Overdrive — Setting

An original superhero setting that borrows freely from popular teams and characters. It is a sandbox: a place to play with favourite characters adapted into one shared world.

## Status key

- **Canon:** written by the author, or confirmed by him in review.
- **Proposed:** drafted in review and not yet confirmed. Treat as a strong default, not settled.

## Standing memory (proposed)

The always-on world block is bespoke, not tied to any template's fields. It carries only what is not already covered elsewhere: no place descriptions (the gazetteer below covers those) and no crew list (the factions list covers that). The author cut a draft Crews section; the three below are otherwise as drafted.

**Mythic Overdrive**
> A sandbox set in the rebuilt world after a collapse. The protagonist can start in any city or crew, and can join, fight, work for or ignore any of them. No crew is the main one.

**Powers**
> The Monster Gene gives carriers powers, and gives some of them non-human bodies. Powers run in tiers, and each one has a limit or a cost. A carrier the state classes as Dysregulated is taken into containment. Hard-light emitters, mostly made by Blacklight Industries, arm the powered and unpowered alike. Old Magic is real and traded in Hargrave. The precursors who built Aurum are gone; Arbiter is the only one confirmed alive. Monstrous hybrids roam the wasteland.

**Licences and Containment**
> The Standings licenses and ranks heroes from D to S. Rank sets pay and sponsorship and decides which incidents a hero may attend, and only the Premier League deploys above B-rank. Carriers judged dangerous are placed by court order at Solaris Academy, held in containment, or sent to Tartarus, where some are fielded as the Chain Gang under explosive collars.

## Canon decisions

- **Sandbox (canon).** Anyone can jump in anywhere. There is no fixed protagonist and no main crew.
- **Powers are re-skinned on purpose (canon).** Characters keep their source's personality, relationships and role, but their powers are changed to fit the setting and keep it original. Do not restore source powers.
- **Every character has a source (canon).** Characters are adapted from named existing characters rather than built from scratch, so they start from a proven foundation.
- **The world in brief (canon, from the module text).** Three cities joined by the Spine, the one highway that survived the collapse: Hargrave, a vertical city four strata deep over pre-collapse ruins, where non-humans hold civil office; Aurum, the capital, built on intact precursor infrastructure, which licenses every hero; Solaris, a solarpunk farming city on the frontier rim. Between them lie the Scrapland Corridors, irradiated country with no licensing authority and no rank system.

## World rules (proposed)

Short standing rules for anything that generates new people, places or groups.

- Call anyone with the Monster Gene a carrier. "Meta" is purist slang. *(The module currently uses carrier, meta and mutant interchangeably; this split is a proposal.)*
- Give every power a limit or a cost.
- Give heroes a one-word codename and civilians an ordinary name.
- Create no new precursors. Arbiter is the only one confirmed alive, and Corona is his only child.
- Abaddon is the only named Chaos Agent.
- Tartarus has no public existence.
- Give no place, person or group a history with the protagonist.
- Write women attractive.
- Use numbers only where a scene depends on them.


## Gazetteer

Every place in the module, nested by containment, with its one-line description as drafted. These descriptions are canon unless flagged under Open setting issues at the end.


### Hargrave
*city.* The vertical metropolis, four inhabited strata deep, built over pre-collapse ruins nobody has mapped. Non-humans hold civil office here, which is what drove the purists underground.

- **The Spires** (district). Hargrave's corporate top tier. The towers run their own weather and sunlight is billed as an amenity.
  - **Blacklight Industries Tower** (corporate tower). Owned by Blacklight. Third-tallest tower in the Spires and head office of the firm that manufactures most of the world's hard-light emitters.
    - **Hard-Light Division** (research floors). Floors forty through forty-four, where the hard-light platform is designed, tuned and serialised before it ships.
    - **The Sub-Basement Dock** (loading dock). The building's freight entrance, four levels under the lobby, and the only part of it with no camera coverage.
    - **The Penthouse** (residence). Blacklight's registered address, on the top floor of his own building.
- **The Neon** (district). The mid-levels, where most of Hargrave lives and works. No daylight reaches them, so the city runs on signage, awning strip and holo-hoarding.
  - **Grand Slam Lanes** (arcade). Owned by Glitch. A Neon-level entertainment complex with bowling lanes, a banked roller-derby track, cabinets and a bar. The exterior sign reads RAND SLA LANES.
    - **The Floor (Grand Slam Lanes)** (public floor). Grand Slam's public space: three working lanes out of eight, the derby track, and the bar that pays for both.
    - **Cherry and Remmy's Room** (quarters). The old manager's office, stripped and rebuilt for two.
    - **The Bunkroom** (quarters). Converted skate storage. Isabelle and Vigga sleep here.
    - **Glitch's Office** (office). The arcade's books, its parts inventory, and the cot Glitch sleeps on.
  - **The All-Night** (noodle counter). A twelve-stool counter off the main Neon strip that has never closed. Half the city's cast eats here, rarely at the same hour.
  - **The Fringe Shop** (occult dealership). Owned by Morgana. Morgana's storefront at the edge of policed Neon, dealing in Old Magic materials, components and information.
    - **The Back Room** (warded room). The warded room behind the counter, where Morgana signs contracts and settles them.
    - **The Loft** (residence). Morgana's rooms over the shop, reached by a stair behind the counter.
- **The Sump** (district). Hargrave's lowest inhabited stratum. Everything above drains through it and a fifth of the city lives there.
- **The Undercity** (ruins). Pre-collapse ruins under the Sump, unmapped and uncounted. Ultraviolet's hex-grid survey covers about a fifth of it and is the only survey there has ever been.
  - **The Darkroom** (base of operations). Owned by Blacklight. A pre-collapse structure two levels into the Undercity, refitted as the operating base of Blacklight's vigilantes. Access runs up through Blacklight Industries and nowhere else.
    - **The Map Table** (operations). Ultraviolet's station and the only complete survey of the Undercity in existence. The family's grid assignments are cut from it nightly.
    - **The Range** (training). Live-fire and close-quarters floor, plated for kinetic armour and hard-light both.
    - **The Bunks** (quarters). Three bunks, three lockers, one shower. Umbra, Eclipse and Ultraviolet sleep here.
- **Hargrave Bay** (coastal water). The water off Hargrave's eastern edge. Commercial shipping is routed around the central channel by an order the harbour authority has never explained.
  - **Tartarus** (black-site prison). Owned by Provost. A sub-oceanic containment facility on the floor of Hargrave Bay, holding prisoners too dangerous for regular custody. It has no public existence.
    - **The Block** (cell block). Tartarus's holding tier, built for monstrous physiologies rather than human ones.
    - **Provost's Office** (administration). The room where Chain Gang assignments are written and the submersible schedule is set.

### Solaris City
*city.* A solarpunk agricultural metropolis on the frontier rim. It feeds the inner territories and stands between them and the wasteland.

- **The Risers** (agricultural district). Solaris's growing towers — forty-storey vertical farms with mirrored light-wells down their cores. The city's skyline is agriculture.
- **The Terraces** (residential district). Solaris's stepped housing grid, built between the Risers and irrigated off the same system.
- **Solaris Academy** (campus). Owned by Solaris Academy. A rehabilitation centre and sanctuary for young Monster Gene carriers and mutants. Most residents are placed by court order rather than enrolled.
  - **The Pit** (training floor). Harrow's training floor, sunk two levels below grade with walls rated for A-rank impact.
  - **The Commons** (refectory). The Academy's refectory and common floor. Staff and students eat at the same tables, which was a policy decision.
  - **Panacea's Clinic** (medical suite). The Academy's medical suite, where volatile genetic codes are monitored and temporarily dampened.
  - **The Director's Office** (office). Halcyon's office, and the room where placement decisions and anchoring sessions both happen.
  - **The Staff Wing** (residence). Staff quarters over the east classrooms. Harrow, Halcyon, Panacea and Meridian all live on site.
- **Farsight Hangar** (hangar). Owned by Farsight. Farsight's base on the wasteland edge of the city, past the last Riser. Sized for the crawler and sited where a bad return doesn't take a neighbourhood with it.
  - **The Aster Workshop** (workshop). Elias Aster's working space off the hangar floor, where the survey data and the release-rate figures live.
  - **Crew Quarters** (residence). Four rooms off the hangar floor. The Asters have three of them.

### Aurum City
*capital.* The capital of the rebuilt world, built on intact precursor infrastructure. Low crime, high surveillance, and the seat of every institution that licenses a hero.

- **The Concourse** (central plaza and transit). Aurum's central plaza and transit interchange, and the most photographed ground in the world. Every low-rank incident in the capital happens within sight of a camera here.
- **The Standings** (ranking authority). The body that ranks every licensed hero and publishes the rankings — one institution doing both jobs. It occupies a precursor structure nobody has fully surveyed.
- **The Paladin Program** (hero academy). Aurum's sanctioned hero academy and licensing pipeline. Graduation comes with a contract, a rank and a sponsor.
  - **The Training Hall** (gymnasium). The Paladin Program's training floor. Licensed heroes of any rank may book it, which makes it the only room in Aurum where an S-rank and a D-rank share a floor.
- **Premier League HQ** (response headquarters). Owned by The Premier League. Operational base of the government's elite response team, in precursor architecture on the capital's central axis.
  - **The Public Lobby** (lobby). The ground floor the public can tour on Thursdays, with a merchandise counter and a wall of ranked portraits.
  - **The Deployment Floor** (operations). Where League callouts are graded, assigned and dispatched. Everything above B-rank in the world routes through this room.
  - **Containment** (holding suite). Kraken's quarters and the suppression apparatus. He is kept here between deployments, awake.
  - **The Residences** (quarters). League housing on the upper floors of headquarters. Living on site is in the contract.
- **The Undercard Storefront** (licensed office). Owned by The Undercard. A sponsored ground-floor hero office on a retail street, with a window display, a bell over the door and a merchandise rack.
  - **Upstairs** (shared flat). Two rooms and a kitchen over the storefront, occupied by four people on a team salary that supports two.

### The Scrapland Corridors
*wasteland.* The irradiated country between the cities. No licensing authority operates here and no rank system applies.

- **The Spine** (highway). The main corridor between Hargrave, Solaris and Aurum. Pre-collapse roadbed, mostly intact, and the only route a wheeled vehicle can take at speed.
- **The Marrow** (trade post). A frontier trade hub built inside the ribcage of a long-dead kaiju, off the Spine. Dust-choked, unlicensed, and the last resupply before the deep wastes.
  - **The Trade Floor** (market). The Marrow's market, run between the ribs on tiered scaffold. Scav-tech, water, ammunition, and passage.
- **The Vanguard Party-Wagon** (mobile base). Owned by Vanguard. A heavily armoured terrestrial dreadnought and Vanguard's home. It has no fixed berth.
  - **The Cab** (drive position). The Party-Wagon's forward cab. Ballast drives; the second seat is usually empty.
  - **The Crew Deck** (crew quarters). Bunks, galley and the table where Vanguard argues about targets.
- **The Cradle** (compound). Owned by Zenith. Zenith's ground: a fortified precursor shell off the Spine, held by whichever faction of the confederation is currently ahead.
  - **The Floor (The Cradle)** (assembly). The Cradle's assembly space, where Zenith's leadership argues in front of everyone it recruited.

## Factions

The module carries its faction list separately from Memory. What each wants and what it holds are written first; the gap between them is what the faction is doing.


### Blacklight's Vigilantes
An unsanctioned human-purist vigilante family operating out of Hargrave's Undercity, funded entirely by the tech firm one of them owns.

- **Wants:** Metas out of Hargrave's civil offices, and Abaddon in a cell.
- **Holds:** A private arsenal, a corporate research division, and the only survey of the Undercity in existence.
- **Method:** Anti-meta ordnance and surveillance, applied from the dark and never through a court.
- **Answers to:** Blacklight, and nobody else. There is no second signature anywhere in it.
- **Reputation:** Feared in the Neon, tolerated by the Marshals-shaped hole in Hargrave's policing, and denied entirely by Blacklight Industries.
- **Members:** Blacklight, Umbra, Eclipse, Ultraviolet

### The Bombettes
A chaotic found-family of semi-reformed criminals operating out of a Neon-level arcade they do not own.

- **Wants:** To be a name in Hargrave that nobody attaches to somebody else's.
- **Holds:** An arcade with a working bar, five people who will not leave, and a great deal of unregistered hard-light.
- **Method:** Loud, improvised, and structurally incapable of a quiet approach.
- **Answers to:** Cherry, in practice. Glitch, on the lease.
- **Reputation:** A joke in Aurum, a nuisance to Blacklight's people, and genuinely popular in their own six streets.
- **Members:** Cherry Bomb, Ice Queen, Firefox, Vigga, Glitch

### The Fringe Shop
A single-proprietor occult dealership at the edge of policed Neon, trading in Old Magic materials and information.

- **Wants:** To keep trading, and to keep the terms current.
- **Holds:** Stock nobody else in Hargrave will carry, and a warded back room.
- **Method:** Contracts. Everything is a trade and every trade is written down somewhere.
- **Answers to:** Morgana, and whatever holds her older agreements.
- **Reputation:** Reliable, expensive, and worth avoiding if you can afford to.
- **Members:** Morgana

### Solaris Academy
A rehabilitation centre and sanctuary for young Monster Gene carriers, most of them placed by court order rather than enrolled.

- **Wants:** Its charter renewed, and every child in it kept out of containment.
- **Holds:** A campus, a charter, four staff, and six years of intake data that contradicts the charter.
- **Method:** Anchoring, dampening, instruction, and a great deal of paperwork written to the finding rather than the facts.
- **Answers to:** The Director, and the review board that signs the charter.
- **Reputation:** A model programme in Aurum, a holding pen to the people inside it, and a target to Zenith.
- **Members:** Harrow, Halcyon, Panacea, Meridian

### The Premier League
The government's sanctioned elite response team, headquartered in Aurum and deployable anywhere in the world.

- **Wants:** To hold the S-rank line and to keep the ranking public onside while doing it.
- **Holds:** Four assets, one of them lobotomised on demand, and the only authority to deploy above B-rank.
- **Method:** Overwhelming sanctioned force, scheduled press, and a deployment board that grades before it dispatches.
- **Answers to:** The government, through the deployment board. Arbiter is the face and not the authority.
- **Reputation:** Beloved, ranked, merchandised, and toured on Thursdays.
- **Members:** Arbiter, Technomancer, Vector, Kraken

### Vanguard
A crew of emancipated sidekicks and Academy runaways working the Scrapland Corridors from an armoured dreadnought.

- **Wants:** To intercept what the cities do not send anyone out for.
- **Holds:** A wagon, three people, and no licence between them.
- **Method:** They get there first and leave before anyone official arrives.
- **Answers to:** Nobody. Corona calls targets and Ballast decides the approach.
- **Reputation:** Unlicensed, unranked, and the only response the Corridors have ever had.
- **Members:** Corona, Ballast, Echydna

### Farsight
A chartered expedition team based in Solaris, famous for going into the deep wastes and coming back changed.

- **Wants:** One more funded season, and the seal closed before anyone finds out it is open.
- **Holds:** A crawler, a charter, a public that adores them, and the only complete survey of the site.
- **Method:** Expedition work in the open, and containment work nobody has been told about.
- **Answers to:** Aegis holds the charter. Azimuth holds the numbers.
- **Reputation:** Civic pride in Solaris and a photo opportunity in Aurum.
- **Members:** Azimuth, Aegis, Relic, Wick

### The Undercard
A licensed D-rank hero agency operating out of a sponsored storefront on an Aurum retail street.

- **Wants:** A rank high enough to cover the rent, and a second sponsor on the window.
- **Holds:** A licence, a storefront, four people, and a merchandise rack.
- **Method:** Get to the scored incident before the rival team does, on camera.
- **Answers to:** The Standings, the sponsors, and Marlowe, in ascending order of practical authority.
- **Reputation:** A punchline in the rankings discourse and the first responders on half the Concourse's calls.
- **Members:** Marquee, Encore, Ward, Marlowe

### The Chain Gang
A black-site deniable operations unit drawn from Tartarus inmates and fielded under collar.

- **Wants:** Operational authorisation renewed, and results that justify the facility's budget.
- **Holds:** Collared assets, a submersible, and a facility with no public existence.
- **Method:** Deniable insertion by people who cannot refuse the assignment.
- **Answers to:** Provost, and a department that has never been named in a filing.
- **Reputation:** Does not officially exist. Every agency in Aurum knows the number.
- **Members:** Provost, Solvent, Yield

### Zenith
A loose confederation of Monster Gene supremacists and unaffiliated radicals holding a fortified precursor shell in the Corridors.

- **Wants:** Recognition that carriers are the successor species, and the resources to act like it.
- **Holds:** The Cradle, a recruiting reach across three cities, and more money than coordination.
- **Method:** Public argument, private drilling, and operations run by whichever bloc got there first.
- **Answers to:** Nobody, structurally. Empyrean holds the room and Attrition holds the largest bloc.
- **Reputation:** The public face of every carrier panic in Aurum, and the only organisation that will take a carrier with a record.
- **Members:** Empyrean, Attrition, Strike

### The Chaos Agents
A network of shapeshifters and nihilists dedicated to destabilisation, of which exactly one member has ever been identified.

- **Wants:** Unknown, and possibly nothing. Settled things come apart and that appears to be sufficient.
- **Holds:** No known assets, no known premises, and resources that have never been traced.
- **Method:** One person at a time, in their own life, with an offer.
- **Answers to:** Unknown. Abaddon has never claimed to lead it.
- **Reputation:** The name attached to every unexplained collapse in the rebuilt world, whether or not anyone was there.
- **Members:** Abaddon

## Undefined canon

Terms the module uses that nothing yet defines. These are the author's to decide.

- **The collapse.** What it was and when.
- **The Monster Gene tiers.** The module mentions a "physical tier" and "S-rank" physiology; the tier list itself is not written.
- **Dysregulated.** Used as a legal class (Vigga was "a Dysregulated carrier bound for Tartarus"); what triggers it is not written.
- **Precursors.** Who they were. Arbiter and Corona are the only living links; Panacea believes they were "simply people who died."
- **Old Magic.** Only Morgana's contract method is shown; that it is all contracts is not established.
- **The seal and the release rate.** Farsight's secret: a site in the deep wastes that is open, leaking at a rate Azimuth recalculates yearly. What leaks is not written.
- **Hybrids.** Monstrous animals of the Corridors; Echydna believes they were once somebody's animals.
- **Non-human bodies.** Vigga is wolf-kin, Glitch a cyborg, Kraken grey-skinned and enormous, Attrition heavy-boned; there is no list of kinds.
- **The Marshals.** Mentioned once in the Vigilantes' reputation; not defined anywhere.

## Open setting issues

Prose problems found in the existing place and faction text. Fix when those entries are next revised.

- The Vigilantes' reputation says "the Marshals-shaped hole in Hargrave's policing": a comparison, and the Marshals are undefined.
- "A fifth" appears twice, for the Sump's population and for the Undercity survey: invented counts.
- "Nobody has mapped", "nobody has fully surveyed" and "the only survey there has ever been" (Hargrave, the Standings, the Undercity): totalizers.
- The All-Night says "half the city's cast eats here", which is narrated story function, plus "has never closed" (a totalizer).
- Two places are both called The Floor (Grand Slam Lanes' public floor and the Cradle's assembly hall). One needs renaming.
- The Fringe Shop and Solaris Academy each have their description duplicated almost word for word in their faction entry.
