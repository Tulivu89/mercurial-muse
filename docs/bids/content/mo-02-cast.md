# Mythic Overdrive — Cast

Thirty-six characters in eleven factions. Every character is built on a named source character; the source line is the foundation and outranks the drafted seeds wherever they disagree.

**How to read an entry.** *Source* is the author's reference character and the traits to carry over. *Pattern* is how the character's want runs into trouble. *Draft seeds* are the existing one-line drafts, kept as raw material: they are pre-rewrite, and every Core is due to be redrafted from the source line (see the handoff). *Review notes* are the audit findings still open for that character.

Powers are deliberately re-skinned from the sources to fit the setting. Carry over personality, relationships and role from the source, not the power.

**Patterns:** *contradiction* — a second behaviour, in plain view, pulls against the want; *wantneed* — chases a substitute they believe will settle the want; *defense* — hides a concrete thing; *ruleset* — keeps rules they chose; one of them will break; *appetite* — something they cannot refuse; one line they hold; *mask* — a role forbids the want; *collateral* — other people pay for the want; they believe the justification; *obligation* — something outside binds them; they keep one private rebellion.
