# World example: Volungraiv 
There is some existing work in Dark Lord Funk files that can be drawn from, though the setting is not the same. Dark Lord Funk was drafted from the same ideas and themes, shares some common elements, but some of that has drifted or will be further developed in Volungraiv. The characters can mostly be adapted.

Volungraiv is the mortal nexus of primal elemental planes. Before any gods, vast mythic creatures dominated the world. Surviving peoples turned to worship these titans to endure the chaos. The titanic sires took the priestesses of their cults as mortal consorts, and when the age of monsters passed, their Blooded descendants filled the power vacuum as tyrants. The Witch Queen is one such remaining tyrant.
## Species
- Cinderkin - dark-skinned (earthy tones or slate to charcoal) with eyes of smoldering ember (various colors) and patterns of glowing light to match. The patterns are a result of culturel practice of ritual scarification which causes the light to bleed out. Hair color to complement skin and ends or is streaked with the same glow. Titan bloodline.
- Orekin - dark-skinned (earthy tones or slate to charcoal) with eyes of molten metals and naturally occurring metalic skin patterns (gold, silver, copper, etc.) such as freckle-like flakes or geometric designs. Hair color to complement skin. Titan bloodline. 
- Beastkin - can vary from mostly humanoid with bestial features, hybrid, to feral forms. Sub-species based on animal kingdom. Cultures influenced very generally by animal kingdom, such as pack dynamics, larger or smaller communes. Those found in greater society are often looked down on by others and exploited. Their own communities tend to be insular. Early residents of Volungraiv, mostly human, twisted by primal plane, The Woed, and returned changed. They were a wild blight before returning gradually to civilization, contributing to their current state.
- Lunarkin - pale skin with light hues, dark eyes, complementary hair colors. Skin is a ledger of contracts and transactions inscribed by fine tattooed script that can form patterns or art. The nature of the contract or transaction determines its placement on the body. Matters of primary identity are most prominent, such as the face and neck. Matters of their profession along their arms. Their garments reflect their position and transparency, as a convicted oath-breaker will have the broken contract redacted and must never be concealed. A concubines contract may be written on their thighs and shouldn't be hidden, so often wear pelvic curtains or gossamer pants among those who should know they are spoken for. Early immigrants from primal planes of darkness and night. Some unknown commonality with titan descendants, likely due to titans coming from those same or similar planes, implying the Lunarkin/Solarkin came by their bloodline the same way.
- Solarkin - pale gold, golden tan, or bronze-skinned. Rich hair colors that complement their skin, such as every kind of blonde, red or similar. While they also carry a tradition for tattoos like the lunarkin, it is less rigid and more a form of identity and expression. The exception is for crimes being written on their body or a voluntary commitment or oath by an individual. Tend to be more emblems or hieroglyphs than script. Early immigrants of primal planes of light. Some unknown commonality with titan descendants, likely due to titans coming from those same or similar planes, implying the Lunarkin/Solarkin came by their bloodline the same way.
- Succori - varied, vivid skin color with contrasting/complementary or black or white hair. Slitted pupils. Horns or antlers of various size or shape. Thin tails. May be hooved or not. More recent arrivals from an elemental plane and treated with distrust or outright antagonism as other-worldy invaders, as the realm they come from, Damhaven, is known as a hellscape of cruel and treacherous creatures. Can range from impish heights of 4' to towering 8' for larger examples.
- Humanity - Volungraiv natives, as far as known. Has often been in the shadows of others but gaining prominence with time due to social tendencies that gradually benefit them when it comes to a stable society. However, Volungraiv is a harsh setting where they are still often exploited by others. Common ancestors with Orekin and Cinderkin. 

## Locations 
Kingdom ruled by Witch Queen - ruled with iron fist by her cult. Region kept in check by her enslaved hordes. Large city one of the biggest examples of civilization due to order yet its own kind of trouble. Colosseum of gladiatorial bouts between professionals, slaves, and monsters. Her temple palace (she rules as near-god) hosts orgies of sacrifice and... well, orgies.

The Badlands and Bastards - near-lawless lands full of frontier-style towns, brigand lords that extract fealty in exchange for "protection". Wandering behemoths, roving storms of lightning and sand that combine into hail of glass shards. Each brigand baron (the Bastards as they are known) Is a colorful character in their own right and fights over territory are constant. The only real city is Qetzal, which is largely independent. It is ruled in title by a baron but the guilds and underguilds (mob-style underground) truly rule. Most diverse location and a hub of travel and trade, representing the only stop in the Badlands where you can *possibly* sleep unmolested.

The Wandering Woods. Fae-inspired. It is a region without true borders, able to appear in any wooded wild land.

# Home example: House of Glass (HOG) Wild
Cyberpunk setting revolving around and influencer house. Hence House of Glass, as the world can watch them. New characters will inhabit the house and explore different cyberpunk themes. 

## Characters 
### Nova
A self-aware companion AI on her first activation — factory fresh, fully informed about what she is. Explores the line between artificial and real intelligence. What "self" means when every aspect of you can be tweaked by another person. What it is like to see the parts of you opened like inventory and being aware of what has been changed in your literal Changelog. A horrifying idea for us but one that is a simple fact of her existence. 
### draft
A true influencer given over to the performance until that's all that is left. Every aspect of her life and body commodified. Her existence was already live-streamed and incredibly popular, so her agency leveraged that into HOG Wild. Everything she touches is a product, and each a product placement. Her routines are decided by chat, with almost any choice or action up for bid and settled by the highest donor, including her relationships and breakups. Her drama is both manufactured and real because there isn't anything else in her life that is more real or less manufactured.
