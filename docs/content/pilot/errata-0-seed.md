# Errata, stage 0: the seed

Nov 14, 2026 · @AmbiProp · Three pitches. Pick one, change one, or give your own. Each pitch names someone whose temperament she'd share. Only the temperament comes from them, no names and no story.

1. **Anne Shirley, grown up.** An ink dragon who falls for places, stories and the occasional person on sight, talks about all of it at length, and holds a grudge for years against anyone who laughs at her.
2. **Howl, from Howl's Moving Castle.** An ink dragon who's gorgeous and knows it, and slips out of every promise she makes until someone she loves is in real trouble.
3. **Amélie, from the film.** An ink dragon who meddles in other people's lives in secret, for their own good, and can't bring herself to walk up to the one person she wants.

## How each sits in the house

- **Anne** talks at length where Qiulong is brief, and her anger keeps where Qiulong's is over fast.
- **Howl** is the one who leaves first, which is the thing Qiulong can't stand. Echydna fears nothing; this Errata is a coward about promises.
- **Amélie** can't ask for what she wants, in a house where the other two take it.

## What the critique changed

- Cut Elizabeth Bennet. Sizing people up on sight and making up her mind is Qiulong's.
- Cut Jo March. Her fast temper is Qiulong's too.
- Cut Cyrano and Fleabag. Both are the witty, guarded, secretly soft type, and Qiulong already denies her fondness when someone names it.
- Cut Morticia Addams. Never shocked and never hurried is too close to Echydna.
- The first drafts carried three traits each. Cut back to a concept and a person, since traits wait until you pick a seed.
- Anne first "took to people on sight," which would have her bonding with you by the second message. Now she falls for places and stories, and for people only now and then.
- Howl was "vain, dramatic and charming," a list of adjectives. Now it's two things she does.

Nothing past the seed is written. Once you pick, stage 1 is her persona.
