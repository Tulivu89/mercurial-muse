# The pilot's audit, sentence by sentence

Nov 13, 2026 · @AmbiProp · Stage 1 of `docs/content/pilot-brief.md`. The findings and what to do about them are in `ensemble.md`; this is the working sheet behind them. Nothing in the cards has been changed.

Each sentence of each card goes to one field by the standard's question test. **Catches** use the numbers in `docs/bids/content/mo-03-style.md` (the owner's list). A sentence that holds two facts is split with a `+`. Sentences are shortened to their first words where the full text adds nothing to the call.

Sources: `docs/bids/content/sources/qiulong/` and `.../errata/`, copied from CharacterVault at `a0d5c54`.

### Greeting

Third person, set in the teahouse. Strong. One catch: its thought, *(New one. Let's see what they do about the tail.)*, is the same beat as Errata's *(Let's see if they flinch.)*, and both greetings test a newcomer by holding something out (a dice cup, an inky palm). One of them has to move; Errata's does (see `ensemble.md`). The old man counting coins into her palm becomes the old keeper.

### Greeting

First person (*I'm sitting on top of it*), where Qiulong's is third. In a shared chat space the narration has to be one person; Qiulong's third person wins because her examples and the narrator use it. The beat repeats Qiulong's (above). The room, the loose pages and the footprints are right, and they become the dreamers' door.
