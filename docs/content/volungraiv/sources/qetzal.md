Nov 18, 2026 · @AmbiProp · A worked first draft of a settlement sheet (`docs/content/sources/README.md`), written before the owner's answers to the plan's questions. Everything here is a proposal. It shows what the method gives, and it doubles as the module process's checkpoint 1: where the slice should be.

**Finished Nov 19, against the town kit:** the questions are re-aimed at collecting, both open re-rootings have an answer, and *The town kit* below fills every row twice: for Qetzal whole and for the slice. The owner picked the gate quarter for the slice (answer 8). Read with the atlas (`../atlas.md`).

## The bar

What the sources do well, which Qetzal has to match:

- **Everyone has an angle.** In the source crowd, nobody in the room is only scenery. Every named person in Qetzal is a use, and an unnamed crowd is in Atmosphere.
- **The law is a business.** The city's order is paid for, and the people in it know the price. Prices on the Peace, the water and the gate are written down, not left vague.
- **The city lives because outsiders need it.** Every threat from outside has a reason to want Qetzal whole.

### The slice in Qetzal, in outline

Lines only. This is a preview of checkpoint 3, so the owner can see what the pick commits to. Updated Nov 19 to fill the town kit.

- **Places:** the gate quarter, holding the gate and its toll house, the caravanserai, the market under its awnings, a storm cellar, the counting-house where the Peace is inked, and the small pit; below it, the Marrow's mouth; outside it, the last stretch of the caravan road. That's eight against the process's four to six, and checkpoint 3 cuts. The pit and the market are the likeliest to merge.
- **People (one of each use):** the gate-warden, who sells the Peace and keeps the caravanserai (a door); a Lunarkin inker at the counting-house (a lever); an underguild collector, with a beastkin gang behind him (a threat with a tier); a pit fighter for sale (a prize); a Succori trader who wants a Compact seat (a mark); and the one the thread turns on.
- **Kin shown:** Lunarkin and Succori, a member of each among the people.
- **Groups:** the underguilds (a faction, with standing); Lunarkin and Succori (species); the Golkin gang in the Marrow (a gang, for A2).
- **The thread:** checkpoint 2 offers three, with a recommendation.
- **Items:** the Peace token (glass, on a cord); a forged contract; storm glass, sold by the weight; the thing the milestone turns on.
- **Lore:** the storm bell (a custom); the titan under the city (a rumour).
- **Scenario:** the caravan reaches the gate at evening, a storm on the horizon.
