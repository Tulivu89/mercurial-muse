## The bar

What the sources do well, which Qetzal has to match:

- **Everyone has an angle.** In the source crowd, nobody in the room is only scenery. Every named person in Qetzal is a use, and an unnamed crowd is in Atmosphere.
- **The law is a business.** The city's order is paid for, and the people in it know the price. Prices on the Peace, the water and the gate are written down, not left vague.
- **The city lives because outsiders need it.** Every threat from outside has a reason to want Qetzal whole.

### The slice in Qetzal, in outline

Lines only. This is a preview of checkpoint 3, so the owner can see what the pick commits to.

- **Places:** the gate quarter, holding the gate and its toll house, the caravanserai yard, the market under its awnings, a storm cellar, the counting-house where the Peace is inked, and the small pit.
- **People (one of each use):** the gate's toll-keeper (a door), a Lunarkin inker at the counting-house (a lever), an underguild collector (a threat with a tier), a pit fighter for sale (a prize), a Succori trader who wants a Compact seat (a mark), and the one the thread turns on.
- **Kin shown:** Lunarkin and Succori, a member of each among the people.
- **A faction:** the underguilds, with standing.
- **The thread, two options:**
  - **A murder breaks the Peace.** Someone with the Peace died in the caravanserai last night. The underguilds must answer it, and the answer points somewhere they can't afford it to point.
  - **The envoy arrives.** The Witch Queen's envoy is three days out on the road. The Compact has to decide its answer, and every faction wants the player to carry a message.
- **Items:** the Peace token (glass, on a cord); a forged contract; storm glass, sold by the weight; the thing the milestone turns on.
- **Lore:** the storm bell (a custom); the titan under the city (a rumour).
- **Scenario:** the caravan reaches the gate at evening, a storm on the horizon.
