# Qetzal: the source sheet

Nov 19, 2026 · @AmbiProp · Qetzal's settlement sheet (`docs/content/sources/README.md`), checked against the town kit (`../plan.md`) and rewritten from the owner's adjustments of Nov 19. Read with the atlas (`../atlas.md`). The slice is Lowgate, Qetzal's gate quarter (answer 8), and its frame is `../slice/3-frame.md`.

**What changed on Nov 19:**
- **A shelter, not a well.** Qetzal is the stop on the Old Road: walls between a titan's ribs keep out the behemoths, and the glass storms break around its tallest bone. The rain that falls on that bone runs down through the city, and each level lower drinks it after the one above.
- **Koriar's temple** stands in that tallest bone. It's a temple and a gaming floor, and its spire is said to draw the rain.
- **Intrigue isn't this world's centre,** so the Compact and the underguilds fight over who collects, with muscle and money, never by votes.
- **No one is banned from Qetzal who has money.** The camps outside the gate hold the people who can't pay, and most of them are beastkin.

## The bar

What the sources do well, which Qetzal has to match:

- **Everyone has an angle.** In the source crowd nobody in the room is only scenery. Every named person in Qetzal is a use; the unnamed crowd is in Atmosphere.
- **Money is in every scene, and never a slogan.** Somebody's trade is going on wherever you stand, shown in what they're doing.
- **The city lives because outsiders need it.** Every threat from outside has a reason to want Qetzal whole.
