## For the map

Notes for the owner's drawing. The map's sheets are laid out from the place tree below (`core/map/sheet.ts`), and the drawing becomes the sheet's edits.

- The Old Road comes across the Badlands to **the Gate**, cut between two of the titan's ribs, and leaves by **the Far Gate** on the other side of the ribcage.
- **Lowgate** is just inside the Gate, the lowest ground in the city. The Lee is by the Gate; the Awnings are behind it; the Pit is cut into the bedrock behind the Awnings; the Counting-House is on the Awnings' edge; the Marrow's Mouth is at the lowest corner.
- **The Climb** rises from Lowgate toward the centre, through the workshops and past the Succori quarter halfway up, to the Heights and the tallest bone.
- **The Camps** are outside the Gate, in the lee of the outer ribs.

**Re-rooted:** the ribs keep out armies as well as behemoths, so Qetzal can only be taken through its own law. And that law is inked on a living woman's back, so it can only be changed on her.

**The bar:** the Merchant's trial makes the law's refusal cost something real, and ours must too. Refusing Gildra breaks the promise that keeps every other caravan coming, and the ending has to show what that costs.
