## The Lee

**Line.** The caravanserai inside the Gate: a walled yard for beasts and wagons, a common room, and rooms by the night.

**Entry** (Here)
> A walled yard with stalls for beasts down one side, wagon bays down the other and a trough in the middle. Off it is the common room: long tables, a hearth, and a bar Hollis pours at from evening on. Rooms run round an upper gallery. Porters in leather aprons carry, muck out and keep the door.

**Atmosphere** (Here)
> Morning: porters forking dung, beasts complaining. Afternoon: heat, flies, drivers asleep under the wagons. Evening: dung-fire smoke, a loud room, Koriar's dusk band faint from the top of the city. Night: lamps on the gallery, dice, somebody singing flat.

**History** (Named: *empire, wall, gatepost, old road*)
> The yard wall is the oldest thing in Lowgate. It was a road-house of the empire that laid the Old Road, and its gateposts carry a mark worn too smooth to read. Hollis built the gallery and the rooms on top of it.

**The Gate Shut** (When the Gate is shut)
> With the Gate shut, everyone who made it in is in the common room or up on the gallery, and nobody leaves Lowgate by the road. The beasts come in from the bays, the yard shutters go up, and glass grit hisses against them until the bell rings again.

**Why each field holds what it holds**
- **Entry** is what stays true through play: the yard, the room, the gallery, who pours, who carries. The porters are there because they're the people a player asks, and the ones who put bodies down the Marrow drain.
- **Atmosphere** gives each time of day one picture. Ansel's flat singing and Koriar's band are the cast and the city reaching the place without being named in it.
- **History** answers one question a scene could ask (*how old is this place?*) and leaves a seed: the mark on the gateposts.
- **The Gate Shut** is only what changes when the bell rings, and it's the scenario's first evening.

**Rules**

| Rule | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kind | building | One building inside Lowgate: stepping in costs a building's time |
| Finesse | Easy (1) | Slipping about a yard full of strangers, beasts and porters: nobody looks twice at one more |
| Flair | Easy (1) | The yard talks. Getting talk out of drivers over a drink is easy |
| Force | Hard (3) | The yard gate is iron-bound and barred at night, and the wall is the empire's |
| Focus | Average (2), left at the standard | Searching the place is ordinary work |
| Alert | Starts quiet (0), left at the standard | The Lee's staff don't watch guests. Under the Peace, that's the point |

## Tamsin

**Line.** A Solarkin hired knife at the Lee, bare of glyphs, who does buy-out work and drinks in the yard where everyone sees her.

**Appearance** (Here)
> Tall and narrow, long in the arm, bronze skin weathered darker across the hands and the back of the neck. Copper hair cropped to a finger's length. Solarkin, with no glyphs at all: bare forearms, bare throat, bare brow. She sits sideways on a bench with one boot up, so she can stand in one move. Thinking, she rubs the inside of her left wrist with her thumb, where a first crime is inked.

**Personality** (Here)
> Asked what she does, she tells you, says it's legal, and asks if you're buying. Asked who paid, she says she never asks, and talks about the Pit. Threatened, she laughs once and moves a hand out of sight. She asks every newcomer who they've talked to since the Gate. She takes any name the counting-house has inked, and won't touch anyone whose Peace still holds, for any money.

**Speech** (Here)
> A low, dry voice with the coast in it, the ends of words clipped flat. Short sentences, and a long one only when she's naming stars or fighters. She calls a killing "the work" and the one killed "the name", and doesn't say "kill". A question she doesn't like gets a question back. Drunk, she gets quieter and more exact, and finishes other people's sentences for them.

**Background** (Named: *coast, Lightbringers, glyph, grew up, past*)
> She grew up on the coast the Lightbringers hold, where a Solarkin's crimes are inked on the spot and a judge decides what counts. She came up the Old Road young, guarding a caravan, before anything she'd done could be written on her. In Qetzal a buy-out that's paid and inked is no crime, so her skin stays bare.

**Wants** (Named: *paid, fee, owed, Ivo, proxy, gold*)
> She's owed for Ivo. The man who hired her, a proxy who drinks at the Lee, hasn't paid. The first time he hired her he paid in gold that wasn't Qetzal's; she changed it at Koriar's table and saw Gildra's face on it. Collecting means going over his head to Ansel and saying aloud that she knows whose gold it is, and she's worked out that the people who know are the next names in the book.

**The Stars** (Named: *star, stars, sky, steer*)
> She knows the stars the caravans steer by on the Old Road and what each kin calls them, and she'll name them for anyone sitting near her in the yard. It's the one subject she'll go on about.

**After the Count** (When milestone 2 is reached)
> She still sits in the yard every evening, but sober now, facing the gallery stair. She sleeps in her boots with a chair under the latch, and asks Hollis each time she sees him whether anyone's been to the counting-house about her.

**Once the Coin Is Known** (When milestone 3 is reached)
> With anyone who knows whose gold it is, she stops saying she never asks. She'll name Gildra, ask what they mean to do about it, and trade what she's seen for her fee and a safe way out of Lowgate.

**Once Ansel Is Bought Out** (When ending 4b holds)
> Ansel's name is inked now, so he's work. She'll take him for her usual fee from whoever paid for the ink, and she wants the job.

**Her day**

| Time | Where | Doing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning (6 to 12) | The Lee | Asleep in her room off the gallery, late, with the door barred |
| Afternoon (12 to 18) | The Pit | On the Pit's top step with a cup, watching the fighters train and betting small |
| Evening (18 to 22) | The Lee | On the bench by the trough in the yard, drinking where everyone can see her |
| Night (22 to 6) | The Lee | In the common room until late, then up the gallery stair to bed |

**Why each field holds what it holds**
- **Appearance** closes on the bare inside of her wrist. Nobody else in the cast closes there, and it's her kin, her past and her want in one tell.
- **Personality** is all *when this, she does that*. The last sentence is **the line she won't cross**: she kills only names the counting-house has inked. The thread tests it. Ansel needs loose ends tied without ink, and ending 4b hands her an inked name she'd like to take.
- **Speech** has words she uses and one she won't, and no sample lines.
- **Background** keeps only what a scene could bring up. It explains why her skin matters to her without saying so in Personality.
- **Wants** is the human complication the owner approved: she knows whose gold it is, and knowing makes her next.
- **The Stars** is her Aside. It gives the yard a scene that has nothing to do with the plot.
- **The When sections** carry only what changes. After the count she stays in the yard, so her schedule doesn't need a second day.

**Rules**

| Rule | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier | Rival | She can kill you and walk away, and she's one person, not a power |
| Rank | 1 | It's her trade: one of your difficulty dice against her becomes a challenge die |
| Force against her | Hard (3) | She fights for a living |
| Finesse against her | Hard (3) | She watches doors and sleeps light |
| Flair against her | Hard (3) | She has a rule about not knowing, and she keeps it |
| Focus against her | Average (2), left at a rival's | Her open side: what she does gives her away, so working her out is the way in |
| Her approaches | Finesse 3, Force 2, Focus 2, Flair 1 | Close, quiet knife work; she reads a room; she doesn't charm. For A2's opponent moves |
| Purse | 1 (food, drink, a bed) | A rival carries 10, but she hasn't been paid for Ivo. Her want is in her purse |
| For hire | Yes, at a rival's price (10: a blade for a month) | Being hired is her work. Her line still holds: she takes only inked names |
| Carries | A knife (hidden), a leather jerkin | A knife hides. The jerkin is because her work comes back at her |

Experience for beating her, her Health and her Strain are the standard rival's; nothing is stored.

## How these reach the narrator in the adventure today

The adventure plays a Studio world through the converter (`core/convert`). Two things in it change how the slice lands:

- **When sections don't cross.** The converter drops them, so *The Gate Shut* and Tamsin's three When sections reach Chat and the Studio, but not the adventure yet. They're written now because they're the record model's, and the code session can carry them across (below).
- **Named sections are folded into the description.** The adventure sends a present person's description whole once they come up, and a place's whole every turn. So the Named sections are written short (Tamsin's three come to about 230 tokens), and the gold person's Appearance, Personality and Speech are each under 420 characters, the most the adventure sends of each today. That's the size every fill matches.
