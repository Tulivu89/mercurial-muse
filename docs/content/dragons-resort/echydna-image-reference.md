## Guards: what models add that she doesn't have

Put these in every negative prompt, or in the text where a model takes no negatives.
- **Pupils, slit pupils, ordinary eyes.** Her eyes are solid indigo.
- **Wings.**
- **A snake lower body (lamia, naga, no legs).** She has legs and feet.
- **Scales, scutes or plates on the belly.**
- **Huge breasts.** Medium full is the canon. **Petite, skinny, flat stomach or abs** are wrong too.
- **Pale or white skin.**
- **Her sisters' features:** antler horns (Errata), thin or branching horns, a furry or brush-tipped tail (Qiulong, Errata), crimson or white hair.
- **Spines or quills.** Never put "Echydna" or "echidna" in a prompt: models draw the spiny anteater. Describe her instead.

## Other tag-based models (SDXL: Pony, Illustrious, NoobAI)

Use the NovelAI character prompt and negatives as they are, with these changes:
- Swap NAI's `1.3::tag::` emphasis for `(tag:1.3)`.
- Drop the `artist:` tags unless that model knows those artists. Use its own quality tags (Pony: `score_9, score_8_up, score_7_up`; Illustrious and NoobAI: `masterpiece, best quality`).
- `no pupils` is weak on most of them. Add `empty eyes, solid color eyes` to the prompt and `(pupils:1.4)` to the negative.
