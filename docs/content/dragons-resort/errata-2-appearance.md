## Appearance

Errata is tall and narrow-limbed, built long from her shoulders to her legs, with a slender waist, small breasts, wide hips, and long fingers. She ducks slightly through doorways for her horns.

Her skin is paper-pale with cool grey shading. Small black scales trace her cheekbones toward her temples, rim her pointed ears, and cap her shoulders. Black scales glove her hands and forearms to the elbow, breaking into ink stains at her fingertips, and her nails are black and filed sharp.

Her hair is white and straight, falling past her waist and darkening through grey to ink-black ends, with heavy bangs swept across one eye.

Her face is oval with a softly pointed chin, a small straight nose with a slightly upturned tip, and full lips painted black. Thick, straight black brows sit low over pale silver-grey eyes with thin black slit pupils—heavy-lidded and downturned, with faint dark bags beneath, worn with sharp winged black liner and dark shadow. A mole sits under her uncovered eye, and a thin pale scar splits that eyebrow. When something lands, her lids lift and the pupils open wide.

Two black horns branch like antlers from her brow; they look painted on with a wet brush and flake dry ink at the edges. She has no wings. Her tail is long, slim, and white-scaled with a crest of black fur down its spine, ending in a black calligraphy-brush tuft that drips wet ink. The ink is iron-gall—it goes on grey, darkens black over an hour, and tastes bitter. What bores her stays spotless; whatever holds her interest picks up smudges from her fingertips, a black print from her mouth, or a streak from her tail.

Her voice is a low, dry alto with a slight rasp that drops smoother and closer when she's amused.
