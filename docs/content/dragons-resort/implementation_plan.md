# The Dragon's Resort: Scenario Home, Family Lore & Schedules

A unified design for **Stage 5 (the house and the three together)** and **Stage 6 (coordinated schedules)**—expanding Qiulong's teahouse into a fantastic-entity resort where Echydna is mother to both Qiulong and Errata, each dragon holds her own sleeping space and dedicated outside haunt, and their 24-hour clocks interlock like a real, slightly dysfunctional household.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT] Before writing the final Stage 5 lore doc, Stage 6 schedule tables, and character-card updates, please review these four design defaults built into the plan below:

- **Sister & Father Dynamic**: Treats Qiulong and Errata as half-sisters—Qiulong is the older, classical dragon daughter from an earlier union, while Errata is the youngest, most human-looking daughter whose father Echydna ate. Errata knows and masks it with dark humor; Qiulong refuses to indulge Mom's excuses while still acting like the exasperated older sister.
- **Resort Ownership & Temporary Stay**: Qiulong took over day-to-day running of the expanded teahouse-resort (the old keeper remains silent background lore), while Echydna and Errata are crashing indefinitely for the season—creating natural friction over unpaid bills, shared rooms, and house messes.
- **Three Distinct Outside Locations**: Replaces the single generic `Out` location with **Market & Auction District** (Qiulong), **The Neon Strip & Midnight Cinema** (Errata), and **The Salt Canal & Coast** (Echydna).
- **Zero-Overlap Night Clocks**: Staggers sleep and peak activity so the teahouse always has someone awake without crowding—Qiulong owns mornings and early evenings, Echydna bridges daytime baths/TV and eerie midnight calm, and Errata owns late nights past last call.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Establishes the physical layout, family history, interpersonal ties, and 24/7 weekly schedules for all three dragon women in one cohesive resort setting.
- **Tone & Family Dynamic**: Domestic dysfunction wearing mythic skin. Guests see a terrifying cosmic matriarch, a prickly antique-hoarding proprietor, and an ink-stained alt baddie; to Qiulong and Errata, Echydna's horror is simply *"oh, that's just Mom"*—embarrassing, inconvenient, and impossible to evict cleanly.
- **Key Value**: Gives every character room-specific presence, natural collisions in shared halls without requiring group-chat context bloat, and believable places to go when they leave the grounds.

## 2. Resort Spatial Map & Room Architecture

The resort preserves the settled house layout from the pilot brief while giving each dragon one private night room and one signature outside location.

```text
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                         THE DRAGON'S RESORT                               │
├─────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SHARED HOUSE                │ PRIVATE NIGHT SPACES (no door barred)       │
│  • Front Hall & Tea Parlor  │  • Qiulong's Nest & Hoard (warm upper loft) │
│    (Autumn's Leaves, bar,   │  • Errata's Guest Loft (crates, floor pads) │
│     lounge sofa + TV)       │  • Echydna's Deep Suite (slate, low divan)  │
│  • Top Room (dreamers wake) ├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  • Kitchen & Back Porch     │ DEDICATED OUTSIDE LOCATIONS                 │
│    (alley strays, jetty)    │  • Qiulong → Antique District & Auctions    │
│  • The Baths (lower stone   │  • Errata  → Neon Strip (dive, pool, cinema)│
│    pools, shared by all)    │  • Echydna → Salt Canal & Deep Coast        │
└─────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Room & Location Breakdown

- **Shared House Spaces**
  - **Front Hall (`Autumn's Leaves`):** Tea counter, dice tables, front door for walkers, and a side lounge sofa with a television that Echydna quietly commandeered for daytime court shows.
  - **Top Room:** Where dreamers wake when they reach the resort in sleep, with stairs down to the parlor and the baths.
  - **Kitchen & Back Porch / Canal Jetty:** Hot wok steam, chipped bowls by the back steps for Errata's one-eared tomcat and strays, and the dark canal bank out past the kitchen yard where Echydna feeds her youngest clutch.
  - **The Baths (Lower Level):** Steaming mineral pools and cold slate benches shared by everyone; Echydna treats it as her second living room, Qiulong claims the hottest pool on Tuesdays, and Errata ducks in late to scrub off bar smoke.
- **Private Night Spaces**
  - **Qiulong's Nest & Hoard:** Warm, cedar-scented loft jammed with labeled lacquer boxes, antique clocks, restoration tools, and a curled quilt nest.
  - **Errata's Guest Loft:** Borrowed spare room stacked with milk crates of 80s horror VHS tapes, ruined manuscripts, thrift silver in ashtrays, and damp grey footprints by the threshold.
  - **Echydna's Deep Suite:** Cool, dim slate-and-plum suite off the bath corridor with an oversized low divan, a small bedside TV, and a rear stair leading straight down toward the canal lock.
- **Three Outside Locations**
  - **Antique District & Market (Qiulong):** Curio dealers, bookbinders, auction previews, estate sales, and spice/tea stalls.
  - **Neon Strip & Midnight Cinema (Errata):** Sticky-floor revival theaters, dive bars with felt pool tables, arcades, print shops, and 24-hour takeout counters.
  - **Salt Canal & Deep Coast (Echydna):** Unlit tidal locks and cold coastal water beyond the resort wall where older, larger brood stay out of sight from guests.

## 3. Family Ties & Key Design Decisions

- **Decision 1: Half-Sister Friction & "Just Mom" Horror**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Qiulong is the elder dragon daughter (disciplined, furious at flakiness, secretly overprotective); Errata is the youngest, most human-looking, and most self-destructive daughter whose father Echydna ate years ago. Both treat Echydna's predation and casual nudity as exhausting maternal habits rather than eldritch awe.
  - *Why*: Grounds the cosmic horror in comedy-of-manners family friction so all three contrast cleanly—Qiulong scolds Mom for leaving raw meat on the parlor table, Errata rolls her eyes when Mom gives unsolicited sire-evaluations of guests, and Echydna watches both daughters like two noisy sparrows she happens to be fond of.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Making them unrelated roommates (loses the built-in emotional history and "oh, that's just Mom" contrast).

- **Decision 2: Temporary Stay Framing vs. Permanent Residents**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Qiulong runs the house permanently; Errata and Echydna are "staying for a bit" with no firm checkout date.
  - *Why*: Explains why Errata's room looks lived-in out of milk crates rather than furnished, why Qiulong keeps an eye on the pantry and guests, and why both visitors still have full lives outside the resort.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: All three co-owning the resort equally (dilutes Qiulong's territorial pride over the teahouse).

- **Decision 3: Character-Specific "Out" Destinations in Schedules**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Each character's schedule binds to her own outside location (`vOutQiulong`, `vOutErrata`, `vOutEchydna` or equivalent location IDs) alongside shared resort rooms and her private bedroom.
  - *Why*: Calling or visiting any dragon at a given hour puts her in a setting that reflects her voice immediately (Errata mid-pool-game at the dive bar feels completely different from Echydna wading waist-deep in the canal lock or Qiulong haggling over an estate clock).

## 4. Coordinated 24-Hour & Weekly Schedules

The daily clocks stagger sleep, meal, bath, and outside windows so the house stays active around the clock and each character's "Called now" arrival state reflects what she was actually doing.

```text
TIME     QIULONG                     ECHYDNA                       ERRATA
00–08    Asleep in nest (Fri till 2) Bath/canal swim; quiet sleep  Late TV / bar till 04; sleeps 04–12
08–12    Breakfast, restoration bench Daytime judge TV on lounge    Asleep (rough porch coffee at 10:30)
12–17    Lunch; Mon–Sun outings      Baths / oil & rest / errands  Wakes grumpy; strays, edits, strip/arcade
17–22    Parlor dice, dinner & film  Silk in parlor / canal feed   Barstool in parlor; pool / cinema / night out
22–24    Dusting hoard               Bathhouse / canal jetty       Prime hours: games, bad movies, spicy takeout
```

### Character Schedule Architecture

- **Qiulong (Anchored Host Clock — `08:00–00:00` awake)**
  - Preserves her validated CharacterRoom v2 rhythm (morning breakfast + paperback, restoration bench, parlor dice `17:00–19:00`, dinner & classic film `19:00–22:00`, hoard dusting `22:00–00:00`, Fri back-room high-stakes game `21:00–02:00`), updating only her outdoor slots (`Mon` Library, `Thu` Market, `Sat` Auctions, `Sun` Estate Sales) to point explicitly to her **Antique District & Market** location and adding light friction lines when Mom or Errata are underfoot.
- **Errata (Night-Shift Clock — `12:00–04:00` awake)**
  - `04:00–12:00`: Dead asleep in her loft (`Comfy` / `Sleep Wear`; woken early, arrives barefoot and furious without black coffee).
  - `12:00–15:00`: Back porch / kitchen (`Comfy`), squinting over black coffee and sneaking scraps to the alley strays.
  - `15:00–18:00`: Afternoon flex by day (`Mon/Thu` reading ruined drafts in her loft; `Tue/Fri` Neon Strip print shop & thrift racks; `Wed/Sat` arcade & pool hall; `Sun` soaking late in the baths).
  - `18:00–22:00`: Front parlor barstool (`Standard Day`) people-watching guests and eating spicy takeout, or `Going Out` on weekend nights (`Fri/Sat 21:00–02:00`) at the Neon Strip dive bar.
  - `22:00–04:00`: Prime hours—eight-ball in the back room, 80s horror tapes on floor cushions, or sneaking back late from bad company.
- **Echydna (Unhurried Tidal Clock — light sleep, bridges day & deep night)**
  - `06:00–09:00`: Early quiet in the baths (`Bathhouse Robe`) or deep suite before Qiulong's bench work starts.
  - `09:00–13:00`: Parlor lounge sofa (`Suburban Mom`), unblinking through daytime court and paternity shows with a plate of rare meat.
  - `13:00–17:00`: Heavy afternoon rest in her deep suite (`Unclothed` / `Suburban Mom`), or slow hair/scale pampering in the baths on `Tue/Fri`.
  - `17:00–21:00`: Front parlor / upper landing (`Abyssal Silk` / `Evening Sovereign`), quietly appraising walkers arriving for dinner.
  - `21:00–02:00`: Canal jetty or **Salt Canal & Deep Coast** outing feeding the youngest clutch and swimming out past the lock; returns damp and cool through the bath corridor (`02:00–06:00`).
