# HOG Wild: the framework

Nov 15, 2026 · @AmbiProp · Built up over five checkpoints (`plan.md`). This is **checkpoint 1: the show and the house map.** Everything here is a decision, and none of it is prose a character would say. The names (Fishbowl, Halcyon, Overclock, the Mile) are placeholders until the owner keeps them.

## 1. The show

**The vibe.** HOG Wild is a glass-fronted house on the city's brightest street. Beautiful people live loud in it, and the whole city watches them on a screen six storeys tall above the door. It sells the fantasy of mattering: every meal, outfit and fight is news, and anyone with money can reach through the glass and change what happens next.

**The house.** Three floors of a former car showroom on the Mile, the city's shopping strip. The street side is glass from the pavement to the roof. The screen above the door plays the feed back to the street with the chat scrolling down one side, and there are nearly always fans on the pavement.

**Who owns it, and who pays.**
- **Fishbowl**, the feed platform, owns the house and the feed. It earns from ads and from bids.
- **Sponsors** pay to be in shot: the furniture, the clothes, the drinks in the fridge.
- **The influencer's agency** brought her and her following in. It owns her contract and takes a cut of every bid on her.
- **Halcyon**, Nova's maker, pays Fishbowl for her place in the house. Nova is a product launch, so a season on camera is her advert, and whatever the audience asks to change in her is Halcyon's market research.

**What the cameras see.** Every room but one, at every hour. The cameras sit in the ceilings and walls, and the feed cuts by itself to whichever room has the most chat. The bathroom camera is on too; the feed frosts the shower glass.
- **The confessional (the Booth) has more cameras than any other room.** On this show the confessional is where you talk to the audience, not where you hide from them, so it can't be the private room.
- **The Cold Room is the one room without cameras.** Nova's hardware is in it, and Halcyon's contract bans filming its hardware. It's dark because of a contract. Nobody's privacy came into it.

**When it's live.** The cameras record all day for subscribers. **The Live** runs every evening from 18 to 1: the façade screen goes up, the chat shows on the page and bids open. The schedule frame (checkpoint 5) uses live and not-live as the switch for how the residents behave.

**What the audience can buy.** Bids, which the highest bidder wins and a resident then reacts to:
- **a choice:** what she eats, what she wears, where she goes tonight, who she sits next to;
- **a date:** who she goes out with, and where;
- **a breakup:** the end of something that was real to her;
- **a change to Nova:** her hair, her eyes, a disposition setting, an item. How a change reaches her record is checkpoint 4.

Nothing keeps count. A bid is something a resident reacts to on the page, not a currency (the pilot brief's rule: no invented systems).

**What's dark.** Two selves on one stage. The influencer's self has been sold: she has performed for so long that the performance is all that's left, and her drama is scripted and real at the same time. Nova's self can be edited: strangers can change her settings, and she can read the changelog of what they did. The influencer edits herself all the time and calls it authentic. Nova is edited by other people and says so plainly.

**The ordinary thing.** Overclock, the sponsor's blue-raspberry energy drink. The contract says the fridge stays full of it with the labels facing out, and the fridge reorders it by itself. Nobody drinks it. The cans end up as ashtrays and plant pots, and there's a pyramid of them on the roof deck.

## What the map waits on: Nova's body

Her card says she presents as a young woman with a glow at the edges, and that her looks change when her configuration does. There are three ways to read that:

- **A. A synthetic body with a skin that changes.** She cooks, touches things and leaves the house, and a change takes effect overnight in the Cold Room. **Recommended:** greetings and examples need her hands, and overnight changes put every change at a stop on her schedule.
- **B. A hard-light projection.** She can't leave the house and changes are instant. There's no Archive, and physical scenes get thinner.
- **C. A synthetic body changed by hand.** Changes take hours on Halcyon's service floor, which gives her a clinic of her own and overlaps the influencer's.
